# Location Scout / Rokehan — provisional exported-set workflow

For a real mapped urban quarter as an illustrative video set. [Nantes trial](../../../docs/experiments/rokehan-manga.md)
proves one native GLB/PNG export, import into Three.js, two timed camera views and
a15s local encode. Owner accepted the lighter-street v2; not a general renderer
or geographic-fidelity qualification. Preserve an explicitly chosen native style.

1. **Choose and pin.** Use public location/coordinates and a small200–300m near
   area. Record location, area, display settings, native camera/style and source
   terms/date. Some heights are inferred; compare landmarks before factual narration.
   Check browser performance; automatically reduced detail is an observed limitation.
2. **Export one set.** Through normal controls save a GLB and a native PNG of the
   intended style. Verify bytes/hashes, dimensions and the source credit. A map-link
   or selected far distance does not establish the exported model's extent. Inspect
   actual bounds and whether surroundings were included. Do not bulk-scrape the tool.
3. **Inspect the handoff.** Check geometry/materials/camera/animation rather than
   assuming all settings transfer. In the Nantes GLB, colour materials transfer;
   the manga shader and camera do not. Retain the native PNG separately. Do not
   substitute image panning for the requested3D camera move. Blender import remains
   documented, not locally tested; check availability before selecting it.
4. **Author the video layer.** Existing Three.js can consume the GLB. Use original
   local contour/halftone finishing, clearly distinguished from native Rokehan output;
   do not extract its program/shaders. Drive camera pose by absolute timeline time.
   Test two angles from one set, inspect street-level placement/clipping and record
   model weight. Preserve credit. Animated characters and real sun/shadows require
   their own tests; static exported people/cars are not animation.
5. **Check the encode.** Pin source/renderer/output revisions in the existing journal.
   Inspect full decode, intended fps/size/duration, repeated frame seeking, actual
   parallax, adjacent-frame line/halftone behaviour, cut luma change, readable labels
   and source attribution. Sparse frames and black/freeze measurements do not prove
   comfortable full-motion playback; obtain owner review before style promotion.

Do not put exported models or asset-dependent media in the public methods repository.
Terms permit use within works (including video), subject to map attribution, but
prohibit distribution of standalone/lightly modified export asset packs. Preserve
separate licensing of OSM/ODbL and other credited data. No new install, paid service,
account grant or publication is implied by this recipe. Use only current authorised
sources/terms; retain the exact export since the beta service has no pinned app version.
