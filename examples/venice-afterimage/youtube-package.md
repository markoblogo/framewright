# Afterimage — YouTube package

## Title

Afterimage | Venice Biennale 2019 — Light, Memory & Machines

## Description

Light becomes a body. A letter dissolves into fog. A robot repeats a task that never stays finished. AFTERIMAGE is an original short art film connecting four encounters with media and installation art from the Venice Biennale 2019.

Using three supplied photographs and a GIF, this film moves between works by Tavares Strachan, Jitish Kallat, Marko Peljhan, and Sun Yuan & Peng Yu. Animated camera moves, a travelling thread of light and shader transitions form an editorial conversation between the works, accompanied by English narration and an original instrumental score.

https://www.labiennale.org/en/art/2019/national-participations/republic-slovenia

https://www.guggenheim.org/teaching-materials/teaching-modern-and-contemporary-asian-art/sun-yuan-%E5%AD%99-%E5%8E%9F-and-peng-yu-%E5%BD%AD-%E7%A6%B9

The links between body, message, border and repetition are this film’s interpretation, rather than a shared statement attributed to the artists.
