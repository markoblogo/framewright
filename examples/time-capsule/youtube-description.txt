Pokras Lampas turns a brick facade into a message for future generations. TIME _ CAPSULE is a short art film exploring his calligrafuturist mural at Lennauchfilm in St Petersburg: human handwriting, cinema history and contemporary street art, cut to an original instrumental trap soundtrack.

The mural asks viewers to decode its letters, signs and references to writing cultures across different eras. Its composition follows the building’s brick architecture, connecting the people who once made science films here with those who will read the wall in the future.

According to the artist’s statement supplied for this video, the inscription was written in May 2026 and refers to a studio making science films since 11 February 1933, with more than 4,000 films. These historical details are presented from that statement, rather than as independently verified findings. The supplied project statement is dated 14 July 2026.
