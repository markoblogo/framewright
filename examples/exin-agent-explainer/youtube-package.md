## Title

Claude AI Brain Surgery on a Mouse? What Exin Reported

## Description

An AI agent choosing a treatment and controlling a surgical robot sounds like science fiction. Exin Therapeutics says it tested that idea in a mouse model of epilepsy — with a human surgeon, a supervising veterinarian and an emergency stop still in the loop.

This 104-second explainer looks at what the team reported: selecting a therapy from 19 candidate vectors, using scientific papers to plan a target, operating a motorised rig and checking camera images. It also explains the reported differences between Claude Opus and Sonnet, and why the agent's refusal to retry a movement matters.

The authors say the mouse recovered. That is not yet evidence that the treatment reduced seizures. This video reports the researchers' account; it does not independently validate a treatment or describe a procedure to reproduce.
