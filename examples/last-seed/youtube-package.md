# The Last Seed — YouTube package

## Title

The Last Seed | A Short Animated Film About Hope

## Description

One seed. One last drop of water. A greenhouse running out of power.

The Last Seed is a 48-second animated short about fragile hope and a small chance to begin again. A seed takes root in dry soil, a failing roof mechanism struggles to move, and the weather outside becomes part of the story.

No dialogue: movement, light, rain, mechanical sounds and an original musical score carry the film from loneliness and tension toward renewal.

Created by Anton Biletskiy-Volokh with Blender and Framewright — my project for repeatable, agent-assisted video production.
