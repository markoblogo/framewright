## Method and boundaries

Reuse [actual game captures](disco-coffee-game-overview.md),not the finished
horizontal film with its titles. Freeze source hashes and the shoot/hold outcomes.
Eight cuts per film use manual actor/mug closeups,two-panel cause/effect and a short
wide impact view. Large editorial captions carry the joke with sound off. The
last source moment meets the opening source position for a replay return;the edit
still resets captions/music and is not a proven seamless loop.

Supertonic3F1 synthesises14new English lines on the pinned local CPU runtime.
Measure before final placement:one phrase overlap and two end overruns required
retiming,not word removal or relabelling of new synthesis. The original game audio
is replaced with a new mix of the same owner instrumental and authored non-tonal
coffee/transition effects. Put the musical drop on the visual payoff and duck
music under measured speech. No paid/model-generation call or new PlayMCP credits.

Initial boys finale crop followed the live worm's position,missing the losing
fragments after the explosion. Full-frame review caught the mostly brown image;
the payoff crop moved left and the final encoded checks repeated. Keep that
negative result:portrait framing must follow consequences as well as live actors.

Each final file passes600frames/exact20s,full decode,encoded loudness/truepeak,
voice separation and native/final picture-stream identity.20one-second samples
plus4full-resolution samples per film and full/reduced covers were inspected.
