# Framewright purpose and development path

Owner-adopted direction, 17November2026. [Roadmap](roadmap.md) carries milestones and execution scope. [Internal studio scope](internal-studio-scope.md) includes film, graphics, sound and related creative products.

## Goal and mission

**Goal:** give one author the ability to regularly turn ideas into high-quality films and other visual works while controlling intention, style, cost and the result.

**Mission:** expand human creative possibilities through AI-assisted production, making complex audiovisual work accessible while preserving the author's voice.

Each production should build studio experience, reduce repeated technical work and expand the author's freedom for the next idea.

## Intended mature product

A personal agent-led creative studio that can take a book, photographs, a discovery or a short idea through creative development, production, review and a publication-ready package. The author directs the intent, makes significant artistic choices and accepts the result. The studio progressively takes on production work at the level of autonomy agreed for each project.

Five capabilities define the destination:

1. Understand mood, conflict, rhythm and the intended viewer experience. Propose concrete directing choices.
2. Maintain a coherent world, cast, voices, action and visual language across scenes and episodes.
3. Select and combine generation, code animation, 3D, screen recordings and manual work according to the scene, resources and available devices.
4. Retain sources, decisions, preferences and revisions so a project can be resumed and corrected predictably.
5. Complete familiar formats from one brief and request author decisions at meaningful creative branches in complex work.

The practical near-maximum is a small one-author studio capable of regular explainers, reviews, promos, atmospheric films and longer coherent fiction. Tools and computers can change while projects and accumulated production knowledge remain usable.

## Development sequence

| Stage | Deliverable | Evidence for moving forward | Existing milestone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Production memory | Case-linked recipes and applicable owner corrections used at intake and review | Next real projects show which prior lessons were applied and whether corrections repeated | M1/M2, M4, RV01 |
| Repeatable everyday production | A few chosen formats with reliable speech, motion, aspect adaptations and delivery | Comparable complete productions with recorded owner interventions, correction rounds, active work and waiting time | M3, AUTOPROD-01 |
| Coherent creative scenes | Acting, contact, cast identity, space and action that remain convincing across cuts | Short action scenes accepted in context, then connected scenes and a complete story | M5, REL-C |
| Production feedback | Creative evaluation and effort considered alongside available publication results | Comparable observations by video age, topic and format, with unknowns retained | SOCIAL-01, M3/M4 |
| Reusable studio method | Stable templates, needed handoffs and worked explanations for the book | Real reuse/reproduction and maintained sources;both Framewright and alternative methods explained | M6, REL-D, BOOK-01 |

These stages overlap. Releases should mark a demonstrated useful capability and its scope. The next release version, date and full acceptance scope require selection from completed work.
