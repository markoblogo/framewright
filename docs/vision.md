# Framewright purpose and development path

Owner-adopted direction,17November2026;expanded27November2026 toward a creative agency with agent-assisted operations. [Agency operating direction and staged plan](agency-operating-model.md) distinguish implemented functions from planned ones. [Roadmap](roadmap.md) carries milestones and execution scope. [Internal studio scope](internal-studio-scope.md) includes film, graphics, sound and related creative products.

## Goal and mission

**Goal:** enable an owner-led creative agency to regularly turn ideas and client/partner briefs into high-quality audiovisual and interactive work while controlling intention,style,resources,commitments and the result.

**Mission:** expand human creative possibilities through AI-assisted production, making complex audiovisual work accessible while preserving the author's voice.

Each production should build studio experience, reduce repeated technical work and expand the author's freedom for the next idea.

## Public proposition and audiences

Owner-selected20November2026. Framewright connects **finished films, real production tests and reusable methods**. Its distinction is research-driven production:investigating combinations of tools,styles and technical approaches in actual creative work,and retaining the sources,corrections,decisions and limits that make later productions better informed. The ambition is creative freedom across styles and toolsets,subject to technical feasibility,access,time and financial resources. Comprehensive tool coverage and ability to realise any scene are aspirations,not achieved capabilities. Improvement is through documented human-and-agent review and reuse,not an asserted self-training system. This is a positioning choice grounded in case records,not a claim that no other studio documents its work.

The public landing and README serve three groups:creators/developers adapting open methods;AI platforms/tool makers seeking real production tests and potential ongoing editorial partnerships;and clients commissioning films or exploring a video workflow for their team. The studio is the main identity,the films demonstrate the work,and the open repository makes the process inspectable. Team workflow work is an offering,not evidence of already completed client deployments.

A partner may gain a review/demonstration and,when the results justify it,a documented reusable application. Access,affiliate terms or payment never guarantee a favourable recommendation or catalogue qualification. Keep disclosures,technical results,owner decisions and audience outcomes separate. Public methods do not grant access to private source media,client material or confidential terms. The current owner-selected stage prioritises tool partnerships that provide production access and enable stronger portfolio work;thematic portfolio groups such as crypto,AI,gadgets and games can follow as suitable cases accumulate. A future partner/referral blog is deferred;this positioning starts no outreach,paid work or blog publication.

## Intended mature product

An owner-led creative agency with agent-assisted production and operations,covering audiovisual work,games and worlds. It connects research/tool selection,resource planning,client/partner commitments,communication and own media to the existing creative studio. The production core can take a book,photographs,a discovery or a short idea through development,production,review and an appropriate delivery package. The complete agency layer is a development target,not an implemented service claim. The author directs the intent, makes significant artistic choices and accepts the result. The studio progressively takes on production work at the level of autonomy agreed for each project.

Five capabilities define the destination:

1. Understand mood, conflict, rhythm and the intended viewer experience. Propose concrete directing choices.
2. Maintain a coherent world, cast, voices, action and visual language across scenes and episodes.
3. Select and combine generation, code animation, 3D, screen recordings and manual work according to the scene, resources and available devices.
4. Retain sources, decisions, preferences and revisions so a project can be resumed and corrected predictably.
5. Complete familiar formats from one brief and request author decisions at meaningful creative branches in complex work.

The current production core is a small owner-led studio working toward regular explainers,reviews,promos,atmospheric films and longer coherent fiction. Games/worlds and agency operations extend that core under the27November direction;they are not all qualified or implemented. Tools and computers can change while projects and accumulated production knowledge remain usable.

These stages overlap. Releases should mark a demonstrated useful capability and its scope. The next release version, date and full acceptance scope require selection from completed work.
