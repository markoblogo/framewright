# Vertical Shorts from existing footage

First scoped trial:[two20-second game Shorts](../../experiments/2026-10-17/disco-coffee-shorts.md).
This is a manual production method,not an automatic router provider or a guarantee
of audience response. Other subjects and engines require their own framing/audio QA.

## Shape a standalone short

1. Start with a readable visual question or contradiction. Pick one outcome and
   a short payoff;do not condense every scene of a long overview into a tiny frame.
2. Recut original clean footage. Use actor/action closeups and occasional context
   panels when a single9:16 crop cannot show both cause and consequence. Preserve
   source/outcome facts and record every source range,crop and speed change.
3. Write brief voice beats and a sound-off editorial text layer. Measure generated
   lines before freezing cuts;retain the original synthesis rows and explicit
   retiming. Do not silently overlap narration or cut trailing words to fit.
4. Put musical/visual accents on the joke. Record replaced versus retained source
   sound. Bake static overlays once and use the retained FFmpeg montage route.
5. Check the encoded portrait file,cover and replay boundary independently. Treat
   viral intent as a hypothesis until owner publication supplies audience evidence.

## First-pair delivery contract

Both files:1080x1920,30fps,20s/600frames,H264/AAC48kHz;new English Supertonic3F1
narration,owner instrumental edit,authored non-tonal effects,large text,PNG/JPG
covers,SRT and title/description/tags/comment drafts. Eight cuts per film. The
picture comes from the real game with scripted-input provenance,not generated
pretend gameplay. No new PlayMCP spending.

Chosen essential-text margins:x80..930,y130..1510. Inspect faces,mugs,targets and
loser fragments at full size and at a phone-sized preview. These are editorial
allowances,not universal verified safe zones for every YouTube app/device.
A crop suitable before an explosion may miss the fragments afterward;the first
boys payoff failed for exactly this reason and was repaired.

Final QA:duration/frame count,full decode,encoded loudness/truepeak,voice/music
separation,no clipped speech,source outcome,visual samples around each beat,
cover full/reduced readability and picture identity after narration mux. Record
retained limitations and separate owner listening/style review. First trial's
narration/frame checks passed;both publication URLs are now owner-supplied.
Human acceptance,audience results and independent playback remain unverified.

## Covers and links on YouTube

Official docs checked2026-10-17 describe choosing a frame from the Short through
the mobile YouTube app. The first trial therefore keeps the cover composition
visible at the start and exports the actual encoded0.4s frame as PNG/JPG. Do not
promise a separate long-form-style thumbnail upload without checking the account.
[Thumbnail guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10343433?hl=en).

Shorts description/comment URLs are non-clickable. Retain a copyable game URL;
a related-video link can point to the overview once the owner publishes it.
Do not invent a profile link or a related video that has not been set.
[Link rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en) ·
[Format eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en).

## Evidence after publication

Record owner-supplied URLs with that provenance. Once actually available,compare
viewing/retention/replay/share observations over equal-age windows,keeping exposure
and different story outcomes visible. A two-ending creative pair is not a
controlled A/B test. No viral-result claim follows from technical delivery.
