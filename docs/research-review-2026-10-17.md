## Compatibility and rights uncertainties

[Converter compatibility](https://dev.mirage.app/tesseract/converters/compatibility)
and repository README differ on supported Premiere frame rates. Pin exact revision,
composition features and frame rate in any trial. AEP export is experimental;
linked AEP layers may require Adobe apps for subsequent editing. Open converter
source does not establish subscription-free rendering or complete edit fidelity.

[Suno upload documentation](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/6141569) and
[Stem Cover documentation](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/9819905) describe
relevant paths,but version/account behaviour needs UI verification. Existing
non-commercial/commercial rights constraints remain in force. Do not infer rights
from a successful download or a later paid subscription.

Record unsuccessful,rights-blocked and unsuitable outcomes as well as successes.
Each future test must define its fixture,resource/cost cap,rights,expected outputs
and separate technical/creative acceptance before execution. No ledger capability
is upgraded until those checks produce retained evidence.
