## Udemy findings and verification limits

The attached recommendations are broadly useful,but distinguish mandatory rules,teaching advice and commercial hypotheses. Old support links currently redirect to an unreadable success portal;do not treat that shell as successful current policy verification. The retained16November source and [official historical checklist](https://teach.udemy.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/CourseQualityChecklist_EN.pdf) support30video minutes/five lectures for a complete course. Current [landing-page guidance](https://teach.udemy.com/publishing/create-your-course-landing-page/) distinguishes200word minimum from1,000+recommended description;the old PDF's1,000word minimum conflicts,so use the current specific article and confirm live forms before launch. That article also lists60character title/120character subtitle guidance;the original working title is not an automatically approved publication title.

Current [quality/AI guidance](https://teach.udemy.com/publishing/high-quality-course-content-best-practices/) asks for substantive instructor expertise,originality,accurate edited material and learner practice. The [2024official TTS announcement](https://teach.udemy.com/the-june-2024-instructor-insider/) supports using TTS in principle,but is not a current approval of our voice or complete course. VTT is the intended caption deliverable per the selected brief and prior lesson;actual import needs the platform smoke test. Revenue-share numbers,review-policy wording,image dimensions,account verification,upload limits and current TTS terms must be rechecked against accessible live support/instructor state before their use. No price,review submission,publication,tax/payment change or new terms acceptance here.

The original brief's private draft-upload/playback test belongs after the ready local lesson and eligible existing instructor access. Udemy's separate Test Video Service is a distinct optional submission,not silently authorised by a private draft upload. Learning effectiveness requires an actual learner attempt;clear tests/beautiful video are insufficient evidence. The pilot cannot establish sales or profitability.
