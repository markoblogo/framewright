## Proposed creative route / bounded admission

**Keep the2D cast and role take, build an animated scene rather than moving illustrations.**

1. Peter approaches in an arc, changes arm/leg posture to brake, points, then recoils. Establish3poses with readable transitions. A generation or authored rig must supply actual articulation; prefer a complete shot to fragile arbitrary cutouts.
2. Quixote grips the lance, leans, takes a horse step and commits to a charge. Short anticipation/contact/recovery beats, with hooves grounded. Own staged driving video or authored motion may supply intent; human-motion transfer cannot automatically solve horse mechanics.
3. Mill: stable tower, hinged arms/sails, purposeful looming motion and strong menace silhouette; potentially Sand3D/Blender with toon treatment. Same camera/light/palette must integrate with cast. No ornamental cloud clock.
4. Set: Atlas image-to-world for a bounded camera move, compared with a simpler painted2.5D set. Reject geometry/style drift before investing in a world. Dialogue uses eyes, hands, posture and timed interruptions; LongCat is a later suitable-backend candidate, not a dependency.

First proposed proof is8–12s, both aspects, not the whole episode. Make a storyboard/timing strip with anticipation → charge → threat → recoil. Compare one low-cost available I2V route (PixVerse after live quote, or bounded PlayMCP) with a ChatGPT GIF/pose-sequence route on the **same short Peter reaction**, then choose the successful source method. A separate authored mill mechanism can remain deterministic. Motion-transfer route is conditional paid access, not forced into a free trial. Freeze the accepted reference, initial/final poses, camera, duration, seed/settings if available, actual wait/correction costs and output rights.

Pass: visible joint/body change, recognizable cast, clean fingers/boots, no duplicate horse/limbs, continuous hoof/ground contact, clear threat and motivation, useful9:16 composition. Reject sliding intact poses, camera-only movement, blink-only acting, confused frightened mill, sustained identity drift or unresolved mask repair. Retain failures as method evidence. No broad tool or savings claim from a single successful shot. Pick the next route only after actual access/cost/terms are known; this note does not launch tests or resume production.
