# Motion playbook - useful practices and boundaries

Owner supplied [Florence's article](https://x.com/flxrnc/status/2120207374250049675)17November2026. Complete visible article read in the browser;retained private review under records/florence-motion-playbook-2026-11-17. The author describes nine production lessons and worked prompts. Model,cost and third-party outcome claims remain author reports rather than Framewright validation.

Useful for our current workflow:

- One timing source drives voice bounds,action and sound;absolute-time drawing makes individual frames reproducible.
- Set shot-specific subject size/contact constraints;check both aspects at reduced viewing size. Do not universalise the author's170px rule.
- Inspect stills before the full export,and movement across time afterward. Global freeze detection alone can miss a frozen hero or flag subtle moving wireframes.
- Prefer explicit paths/clearance and grip constraints over fixing intersections after export. A plausible still can hide impossible reach.
- Define completion as actual files,checks and handoff. Keep owner listening/creative decisions distinct;check encoded AAC rather than only its source mix.

[Praxis case](../productions/praxis-robot-engineering.md) applies these ideas now:the numeric arm check caught a real first-pass reach failure. Native finite scenes do not need seamless-loop checks;one-frame audio delay and a fixed damping ratio are design choices,not blanket requirements. Use our existing journals,taste rules,tool records and approved scope. No tool bundle/model/paid CLI installed from the article. No change to Quixote's pause or to release priorities.
