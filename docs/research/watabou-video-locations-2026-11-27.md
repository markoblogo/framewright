## Tools and intended use

- [Medieval Fantasy City Generator](https://watabou.itch.io/medieval-fantasy-city-generator): procedural2Dcity planning. Useful for map reveals,fictional geography and choosing streets/landmarks before a scene. Shapes are artistic,not reliable historical/civic simulation. [Author FAQ](https://watabou.github.io/faq.html) permits map use/modification,including commercial RPG material;attribution appreciated. Do not conflate output permission with the old source repository'sGPL3or assume equivalent permissions for every3Dasset/code use.
- [City Viewer](https://watabou.itch.io/city-viewer): visualize imported city JSON,choose cameras/light/style and export OBJ. Useful for moving establishing shots,street-level perspectives and geometry for further video finishing. It is the modern counterpart for a particular exported map. Built-in settlements are separate examples,not our newly generated map. Author notes that preset changes can regenerate roof/wall details:freeze a selected model for scene continuity. Its generated geometry does not establish a ready-lit/textured scene in another renderer.
- [Toy Town](https://watabou.itch.io/toy-town): older self-contained procedural town visualization,with first-person movement and environment palettes. Treat as the subject of a meditative walkthrough review,not our game-asset pipeline. It embeds an old generator and does not import current MFCG maps. Author documents mouse-turning limitations. Individual contemporary controls still need shot-level confirmation when recording.
