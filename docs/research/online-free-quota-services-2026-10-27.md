## Priorities and current-state boundaries

A specific production selects one candidate at a time: (1) Adobe Podcast for a
cleared speech-cleanup comparison or TTSMaker for a short voice comparison;
(2) PixVerse for an original video shot only after verifying the selected operation
fits available free credits and rights; (3) Meshy for a cleared original humanoid
or prop, followed separately by Mixamo. Clipchamp is conditional on caption need.
Tripo, Suno and ElevenLabs retain prior research; do not create duplicate canonical
capability entries or conflate provider docs with a completed test. Commercial
eligibility, source and model licences remain independent checks. All other items
stay available as named candidates/reference. Stage tests sequentially on the Mac.

The intake prototype can attach three dated snapshots (PixVerse, Runway and Meshy)
to explicitly supplied routes using the optional `provider-terms-pilot.json`. It does
not auto-select services, verify current conditions, query balances,
or compute quota schedules. The future producer-agency catalogue slice must represent
published facts, provenance/date, private entitlement, free vs one-off vs paid class,
reset/timezone, model operation cost and rights independently. Refresh changing terms
at use time; unknown is unknown. Do not silently publish, rank or run these candidates.
