# HKU video benchmark — scoped evidence for provider comparison

2 November 2026. Read-only research; no model/provider call, spend, upload or new
production. Trigger: [ForkLog, 26 October](https://forklog.com/news/ai/kitajskaya-seedance-oboshla-veo-i-sora-po-kachestvu-ii-video).
Primary sources: [HKU release](https://lead.hku.hk/en/article/2026/10/hku-business-school-releases-report-on-gen-ai-video-generation-capabilities/)
and its linked [19-page working paper](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ArNWz3Ru07Hmz7r5r4ihtly4MNVCDsDB/view).
The report's introductory/method/results text on pages 2–9 was inspected in the
browser; no full statistical or generated-corpus reproduction was performed.

## What the result establishes

The university's table puts Seedance 2.0 Pro at 3.84 for visual-only quality,
Veo 3.1 at 3.47 and Sora 2 Pro at 3.27; audio-inclusive results also rank Seedance
first. The report describes Chinese-language tasks, 71 items (50 T2V, 21 I2V),
20 human raters and a 1–5 rubric. Its introduction scopes models to April 2026;
Sora is explicitly a historical reference. It does not evaluate Seedance 2.5.
These are source-reported results, not Framewright measurements or a live market
ranking. Page 7 describes bootstrap confidence intervals; raw rating data and
provider-generation settings were not independently audited here.

There is a numerical error in the university news text: Wan 3.41 → 2.75 is a
0.66-point difference, about 19.4% relative to 3.41, not the stated 66% decline.
The table values are retained; the percentage is not repeated as evidence.

## Implications for our own production — interpretation

**Do not change the default router from a headline.** A dated model name is not
the current endpoint, an aggregator label, an entitlement or an export/rights
check. A leaderboard score cannot establish cost per accepted shot on this Mac.
The existing [account/provider record](free-video-good-dog-2026-10-31.md) remains
the source of observed trials; recheck balances and exact model IDs at actual use.

**Separate input modes.** T2V and I2V scores aggregate different task sets, so they
are not a controlled experiment proving that adding an image itself causes lower
quality. For a persistent Good Dog character, reference fidelity may be essential
even if unconstrained T2V looks prettier. Evaluate beauty, required identity and
action preservation separately. Never drop a canonical reference just to obtain a
higher aesthetic score on a different task.

**Keep evaluation dimensions explicit.** Our proposed review should distinguish
appearance/identity continuity, physical contact and spatial logic, composition,
instruction compliance, sound quality and cue synchronization. Native generated
audio and our separately produced voice/music are different production routes;
silent footage is not automatically a failed shot when the brief calls for external
sound. Technical decode, human creative acceptance, rights and publication remain
separate. The source's safety leaderboard is not a reason to seek a weaker filter
or a replacement for source/rights review.

## Enrich GD-NEXT-08 / existing provider test — not started

One selected original 4–6s shot with known action/camera and, when required, an
approved hero reference. Freeze the shot contract and acceptance criteria first.

- **First provider:** only an existing genuinely free/already-paid eligible mode.
  At most one T2V and one I2V output within a pre-agreed attempt/credit cap. Preserve
  prompt, actual model/version, source hash, enhancement setting when exposed,
  duration/aspect and quoted versus observed charge. If one mode is unavailable,
  record it; do not substitute a different model silently.
- **Optional second provider:** only if the selected test budget and quota allow;
  repeat the same scene requirements, not an unrelated showcase prompt. Label
  endpoint-dependent controls and stochastic output; this miniature cannot estimate
  a population ranking or statistically prove universal superiority.
- **Review:** compare labelled outputs against the scene contract and one baseline.
  Blind model labels where practical. Record accepted seconds, identity/action
  failures, contact, sound sync and editability. Report time/known costs with
  unknowns, not the paper's score or advertised generation speed.
- **Closeout:** selected result and compact attempt/rejection evidence under the
  [storage policy](../storage-policy.md). Do not keep every full failed export or
  start a new cross-provider benchmark fleet. If quota/rights/access are blocked,
  retain the existing footage/local-render fallback and keep the trial conditional.
