## Useful method, independent of the app

An intact object → parts separate on controlled paths → one component highlighted
with an explanation → object reassembles. Possible uses: an original fictional
machine, the Good Dog production-line explanation, a product mechanism or a
media-art transition. Build it around a specific narrative question rather than
a decorative break in continuity.

For factual devices use known diagrams/CAD/verified components or explicitly label
an illustrative concept. A generated hidden interior can be plausible and wrong;
a photorealistic exploded view is not a measurement or evidence of real internals.
Do not describe a generic splat or generated photo as an editable component assembly.

## EXPLODED-VIEW-01 — queued local baseline

M4/R05, conditional R06; possible M5/R07 insert and PARAM-SHOT-01 reuse.
First choose a shot that genuinely needs an explanation. In the existing Blender
workflow, author one simple original three-part mechanism with known components.
A 5–8s shot separates its parts, highlights one and reassembles. Save component IDs,
reference transforms, camera, timing and sound cues. Change only separation distance
and render again; compare with the baseline.

Require readable order, stable part identity/count, no intersections, exact return
to the assembled state, correct depth/occlusion, useful framing in 16:9/9:16 and
contextual listening/viewing. Cap at two previews and one correction. Local authored
geometry is the controllable fallback, not claimed photorealistic reverse engineering.
No new renderer or modelling package is needed for this proposed baseline.
