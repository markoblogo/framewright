# Reverse engineering video — research and experiment intake

25 November 2026. Owner requested a research-led video and useful candidates for the studio. Scope: extract a reusable technical recipe from a finished authorized reference, not recover a lost layered project, identify a generator conclusively or replicate a third party's creative work. Prefer local Mac routes and original reconstructions. No new subscriptions, cloud video uploads, automated remake, or publishing.

## Methods worth developing

1. Establish a ground-truth fixture and keep its original edit recipe. Inspect decoded dimensions, cadence, shot boundaries, reference frames and timestamps before interpreting content. Record misses and false cuts. FFmpeg and the already scoped PySceneDetect are baselines; compare harder cuts and transitions separately.
2. Measure motion using optical flow and global affine fits; distinguish digital pan/zoom from local object motion. Sample palette, luminance/contrast and framing, not a claim of original LUT, lens or light setup. A screen capture displaying 3D can look identical to the rendered source; mark provenance uncertain unless source evidence resolves it.
3. Build a shot card with visual content, timing, motion, sound cues, transitions, overlays, technique hypotheses and manual corrections. Separate observed measurements from inferred production routes. For audio, test beat/transient detection and optional voice/music separation on owned audio; evaluate leakage and timing by listening, not by claiming the original stems were recovered. Current fixture is silent.
4. Serialize a reviewed recipe as VIDEO.md and, if useful, convert only verified boundaries into OpenTimelineIO. Neither format recovers an original editable timeline. Reconstruct a short **new** scene with similar technical grammar and compare to the target attributes; retain differences. Do not import generated prompts or instructions as executable commands.

## Secondary references / no activation

[Video Prompt Extractor](https://github.com/HiAPIAI/video-prompt-extractor): early local scene/keyframe core and optional cloud interpretation; avoid upload until explicit authorized test. [ShotParser](https://github.com/MaanasTaneja/ShotParser): timeline/Whisper/Gemini route, reserve for a source-cleared comparison; provider access and cost not verified. [Video-to-prompt workflow](https://github.com/gpt-img-2/video-to-prompt): reference for visual grammar, shot inventory and semantic prompts, not qualified runtime.
- [OpenTimelineIO](https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/OpenTimelineIO) already has narrow Framewright frame-range handoff evidence. [PySceneDetect](https://github.com/Breakthrough/PySceneDetect) already used for authored hard-cut baseline. Do not duplicate their existing qualifications.
- [NVIDIA ViPE](https://github.com/nv-tlabs/vipe) is an advanced camera pose/depth reference; not a practical current Apple Silicon baseline without a compatible route. No recovered camera or lens claim. [Gemini video understanding](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/video-understanding) and [TwelveLabs](https://docs.twelvelabs.io/v1.3/docs/guides/analyze-videos-and-images/videos) are conditional external semantic routes only, with actual plan/credits, retention/rights and output fidelity to verify. We did not use them here.

Research is based on linked upstream documentation and the one explicitly recorded local test. No independent generalized accuracy, cost, speed advantage, or tool qualification established. The queue is future test work, not a change to the production order or a new dependency.
