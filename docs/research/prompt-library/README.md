## One Prompt Design

[Source library](https://onepromptdesign.com/). Public categories and the previously inspected editorial/coin examples suggest useful art directions. No explicit copying licence was located in reviewed pages. Therefore the cards below are original Framewright prompts, not verbatim imports or licensed copies of gallery images. The source is inspiration/provenance only; independently generate assets. Still-image prompts do not establish motion quality.

### OPD-01 — Coherent book atmosphere

Use: three source frames for a short book promo. Inspired by Classical Cinematic Moments.

> From the approved synopsis and character sheet, design three 16:9 frames: arrival, discovery, consequence. Keep the same character proportions, wardrobe, location geometry and restrained palette. Use directional light and foreground depth to guide attention. Show one clear action per frame. Leave an uncluttered upper-left area for independently typeset title text. Do not invent plot facts. Return a continuity checklist before generating.

Pass: coherent identity/space across all three; readable action at thumbnail scale; owner approves correspondence to book. Next: animate one approved pair, checking camera travel and object stability.

### OPD-02 — Surreal landscape with a camera route

Use: fiction environment plate. Inspired by Surreal Minimalist Landscapes.

> Design an original wide landscape for a 16:9 film with three spatial layers: a nearby textured surface, a distinct middle-distance destination and distant atmosphere. Choose two dominant colors and one restrained accent. Keep a physically traversable route between foreground and destination. Produce a wide establishing view and a closer view of the same place; maintain horizon, light direction and architecture. Avoid text and logos.

Pass: views describe the same location; destination and depth are obvious; route can support character movement. An image is not a reconstructed 3D world.

### OPD-03 — Editorial cover and companion inserts

Use: text-bearing YouTube cover and related illustrations. Inspired by Blue Grain Editorial and the Velvet Editorial series.

> Make an original restrained editorial composition about {approved subject}, using one focal object, generous negative space and subtle grain. Supply a clean image without lettering, with room for the approved short headline. Match the same palette and lighting in two companion inserts. Avoid generic decorative objects unrelated to the subject. Typeset the final headline separately and check it at 320 by 180 pixels.

Pass: headline reads at reduced size; subject is immediately identifiable; inserts feel related without being copies. Owner preference: cover contains text.
