## Existing city tool: Rokehan

[Prior accepted test](../experiments/rokehan-manga.md): Nantes GLB/PNG, two camera angles, original Three.js manga finishing,15s1080p24, lighter-street v2 accepted. This establishes a bounded background route, not character animation or a new town qualification.

The [author guide](https://oyasumi-gamedev.com/tools/rokehan/) currently describes browser construction of real places from OSM/elevation and PNG/SVG/GLB/FBX/OBJ outputs. The previous trial tested GLB/PNG only. It does not generate an arbitrary fictional Bracken Ford from prose. Select a suitable real street as a staging donor or build a small fictional set separately; keep the same junction, store, fence and directions across shots. Native exported geometry did not retain the manga shader/camera in our trial.

[Terms](https://oyasumi-gamedev.com/tools/terms/) allow outputs within works, including commercial video, with required map-data attribution; standalone asset redistribution and extraction/reuse of the service program are restricted. Treat it as a proprietary web tool, not OSS. Save models privately and use normal export controls. A donor street is not a claim about the book's real location.

## Creative continuity

The quieter opening and ending are deliberate restrained movement, not accidental freezes. Differentiate motion language by phase. Bubbles attach to a speaker/source; speed lines respect panel bounds; CRACK!/THUD!/WHOOSH! correspond to events. BOOM! must not fabricate an explosion. Do not invent the unseen regulator collision, rescue or animal intention. The boy is fifteen during the incident; adult archive references belong to the deferred story. Keep the manuscript's losses in the adaptation even with non-graphic staging. Details and full script stay private.
