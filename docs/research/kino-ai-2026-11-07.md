## Fit and evidence

Official [agent documentation](https://kino.ai/docs/working-with-agents) lists Mac Apple Silicon and Intel builds and a local HTTP MCP endpoint (`127.0.0.1:45680/mcp`), including Codex CLI instructions. The app must stay open and signed in. This matches our Mac/agent stack on paper; actual host/resource compatibility is untested.

[Pricing](https://kino.ai/pricing) states Free includes the editor, unlimited imports/exports, monthly built-in AI allowance and **100 metered MCP calls per week per organisation**. Model subscription/usage and generated media costs are separate. Free caps reset weekly/monthly as applicable. Do not interpret this as unlimited free AI, generation, or review hosting. Exact account entitlement remains unverified; paid overages are not admitted to the proposed trial.

The [editor guide](https://kino.ai/docs/kino-for-editors) documents timeline edits, checkpoints/restore, agent edits, alternative aspect layouts and exports including H.264/ProRes. These are relevant to shot assembly, a bounded correction and full-film 9:16 adaptation. Claimed timeline import support does not prove lossless Framewright/OTIO round-trip; that is UNKNOWN.

The same guide says **720p proxy upload is default**, while camera originals stay on the drive; original upload is a separate action. Thus desktop/local playback is not proof of a fully local/private workflow. Before a trial verify proxy/cache location and whether cloud transfer can be disabled. All owned caches/media must stay on Work; current internal free space is too small for an unbounded installation/indexing run.

The [homepage](https://kino.ai/) describes agent-accessible editing, searchable footage, graphics built on Remotion and review comments. Treat speed/productivity testimonials as marketing, not measured savings. It is a proprietary service, not an established open-source replacement.

[Terms](https://kino.ai/privacy), dated 19 October 2026, distinguish content creation from prohibited use to derive a competing/similar product. Use only its documented customer editing/MCP workflow in a trial; no reverse engineering, feature/implementation copying or adoption of proprietary code/templates. Broad boilerplate about automation also needs reconciliation with the documented MCP route before connecting; no legal clearance claimed.

## KINO-EDIT-01

Question: does documented native timeline/MCP editing reduce one real correction/handoff bottleneck without breaking source paths, sound or framing?

1. Preflight: exact version, Mac requirements, Free allowance, documented MCP access, storage/proxy behaviour and account terms. No purchase, generation or private manuscript upload.
2. Use a 12–15s non-sensitive test pack of two retained clips plus a WAV. Keep originals and proven FFmpeg baseline intact. Obtain permission for any unavoidable cloud proxy transfer.
3. Assemble via MCP, adjust one cut and move one caption; reopen the project. Check all requested edits and unchanged audio/content.
4. Export 16:9 and 9:16; inspect faces, object contact, crop, safe text position, sync, seekability, full decode and final hashes. No unsupported intermediate interchange claim.
5. Log actual wall time, MCP calls, bytes/cache growth and any charges against the same baseline task. Pass only if edit/reopen/export work and the observed benefit is useful; preserve failures and owner preference separately.

Cloud review links are optional later, account/plan gated. No automatic publishing, mass migration, install or current-film rerender is authorised by this research.
