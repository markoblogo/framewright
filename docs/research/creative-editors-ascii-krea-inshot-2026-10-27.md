# GLEA, Devault ASCII, Krea plugins and InShot — research intake

Checked 27 October 2026 using vendor documentation. All items are **unverified candidates**, not production qualifications; marketing claims are attributed to vendors. No installation, account setup, generation, credits, subscriptions or publication were performed. Prioritise free/open-source macOS workflows; paid hosts and services stay conditional reserves.

## 1. GLEA / GreenLight Dash

[GLEA editor](https://greenlightdash.pro/glea-editor/) / [editor documentation](https://greenlightdash.pro/documentation/video-editor/). Desktop app for macOS 13+ (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux. Vendor describes a multilayer timeline for footage, audio, text, HTML, 3D models, particles, camera and MP4/WebM output. Useful to compare a reviewable agent-oriented editor with our HyperFrames/Motion Canvas/Blender workflows. Source-code licence and offline capabilities are **not verified**; do not call it open source.

Free basic editor/rendering is stated in the product [FAQ](https://greenlightdash.pro/glea-editor/); PRO assets can require payment to render and external AI calls use provider credentials/charges. **Conflict:** that FAQ and the [agent page](https://greenlightdash.pro/documentation/agents/glea/) say integrated GLEA agent requires PRO; [editor documentation](https://greenlightdash.pro/documentation/video-editor/) calls editor and agent free. Treat agent availability as unknown until verified in installed version / by vendor; no purchase to resolve it.

**Queue:** free editor only, one original 15–20 s horizontal clip with video, title, sound and one editable animation; save/reopen, change one value, render and inspect. Compare editable project/recovery, quality, render time, manual/agent control and portability; keep paid-only agent separately untested. No provider calls. Only install after choosing the trial and checking current permissions.

## 2. Devault ASCII

[Generator](https://devault-ascii.pages.dev/ascii-generator/), [app](https://devault-ascii.pages.dev/ascii-generator/app/), [privacy statement](https://devault-ascii.pages.dev/ascii-generator/privacy/). Vendor describes browser-side image/video conversion, ten styles, 14 presets, ASCII/Braille/dither/CRT controls and MP4 export up to one minute. WebGL2 required; free account required to export. Vendor says source files remain in browser; this is a vendor privacy claim, not an independently verified network audit. Open-source status and output rights for commercial use are unverified.

**Queue:** when Pocket OS resumes, 5–10 s original/synthetic device footage with actual on-device text kept legible; compare ASCII vs Braille with unprocessed footage and existing Three.js AsciiEffect/TerminalTextEffects. Inspect motion, legibility at phone size, timing, 1080p export, file compatibility and watermark; only account setup if needed for the selected test. No existing Pocket OS pause is revoked.

## 3. Krea plugin family

[Full catalogue](https://www.krea.ai/plugins) lists Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Blender and ChatGPT, with separate MCP access. These are provider-connected workflows, not offline/open-source guarantees. [Premiere](https://www.krea.ai/plugins/premiere-pro): cloud-generated plates/inserts and look development imported into the timeline. [Photoshop](https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-photoshop-plugin): generation/relight/background work. [Illustrator](https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-illustrator-plugin): image generation plus Illustrator Image Trace, not a proven native vector generator; Illustrator 2025+. [After Effects](https://www.krea.ai/plugins/after-effects): visual references and motion-design assets; host required. Adobe hosts are paid-product reserves unless already owned.

[Resolve](https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-davinci-resolve-plugin) is useful for grade references/plates but explicitly requires **Resolve Studio**, macOS 12+, Krea account and internet; the free Resolve version is not supported. [Blender](https://www.krea.ai/plugins/blender) generates material/environment/lighting/camera references; it does not replace EEVEE/Cycles or automatically prove editable geometry. Requires Blender **5.2+**, not the 4.5.15 used for Last Delivery, plus online account. [ChatGPT](https://www.krea.ai/plugins/chatgpt) and MCP are alternative connectivity candidates; actual enabled tools and authentication were not verified.

Vendor lists a free tier with [100 compute units/day](https://www.krea.ai/pricing); model availability, specific price, entitlement and ability to use a given plugin remain unverified. The pricing FAQ identifies paid tiers as including commercial rights, and Krea's [upscaler FAQ](https://www.krea.ai/features/ai-upscaler) excludes commercial use for free-user upscales. Model/provider terms can differ; treat free-output publication rights as **unverified**, not automatically cleared. Inspect upload/privacy, use authorised or synthetic source material and validate the exact output licence before use. No paid calls in exploratory testing.

**Queue:** after verifying free availability and rights, compare a single synthetic material/lighting reference in Blender 5.2+ with our existing local workflow: one source scene, one generation, one manual revision and same-camera export; measure consistency, editability, time and cost. If a suitable existing app or authorisation is unavailable, keep the plugin as a reference candidate; web/ChatGPT generation is not evidence that the host plugin works. Adobe and Resolve Studio integrations remain reserves.

## 4. InShot on Android and from Mac

Owner reports an active annual Android premium subscription; its live entitlement and device availability have not been checked. [InShot terms](https://inshotapp.com/terms.html) state subscriptions cannot be transferred between systems. The App Store lists the iOS app as available on compatible Apple Silicon Macs, but an Android/Google Play purchase does not automatically become an Apple App Store entitlement: [InShot's iOS terms](https://inshotapp.com/terms/terms.html) and [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/inshot-video-editor/id997362197) distinguish the platform. Do not purchase an additional subscription without a specific request.

**Candidate A — physical Android + Mac:** [scrcpy](https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy) is Apache-2.0 and mirrors/controls the real phone or tablet through USB or TCP/IP; Android app still runs on the existing device and uses its own entitlement. USB debugging and device approval are needed; playback/audio forwarding depends on Android version/device. Full-resolution original exports must be transferred as files, not assumed equivalent to a mirrored screen recording. Consent, app security policy and compatibility must be checked. No credentials in chat.

**Candidate B — Android emulator:** [Android Studio emulator](https://developer.android.com/studio/run/emulator) can run Google Play-equipped system images on macOS; [AVD documentation](https://developer.android.com/studio/run/managing-avds) describes Google Play images. App compatibility, Play Integrity, GPU/video performance, sign-in and whether the existing entitlement restores inside an emulator are **not established**. This is Android running on Mac, not a transfer to the macOS app. Do not bypass licensing or platform controls. The official documentation lists 16 GB RAM and 16 GB disk as recommended emulator resources; validate actual free space first.

**Queue:** favour a bounded physical-device trial: verify premium on Android → explicitly enable/approve the connection → mirror a synthetic local edit → export on Android → transfer original file → inspect on Mac. Compare speed, edit controls, actual output and privacy. Emulator is a separate fallback trial with no purchase; if entitlement or app fails, report and stop rather than assume support. Nothing has been installed or connected yet.
