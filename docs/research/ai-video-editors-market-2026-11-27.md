Keep Framewright's owner-adopted identity: an independent creative studio and production lab whose films produce reusable methods. Treat a video editor as a replaceable production component. Investigate two additions, **Editroom** and **Daydream**, against FilmCraft/FFmpeg and the current production method. Some also cover review, reusable recipes, publication and budgets. Framewright must demonstrate a useful complete result for a specific audience, rather than claim a unique feature bundle.

## Competitive implications for Framewright

**Proposed emphasis within the existing positioning, not an adopted rebrand:** a production studio for independent creators and small teams making reviews, partner demonstrations and creative films across several tools, with a recoverable package and clear decisions.

A useful promise to validate: *“A finished film, plus the sources, decisions and editable recipe needed to revise or resume it without starting over.”* English wording is a proposal for discussion, not a landing-page change.

The full job is brief → creative approach → evidence-aware tool choice → bounded pilot → footage/generation/edit → review and correction → publication assets → retained production knowledge. Include resource budgets where relevant. A customer may need one film or a repeatable production method; do not turn an internal tool catalogue into the product.

What the scan changes:
- Agent access, preference memory, editable timelines, recipes and exports are now comparison criteria, not exclusive advantages. Daydream, Scarlet, Kino and Takehound overlap with parts of this work.
- Lazycow's production/review/results/budget descriptions and useDailies' brief-driven checks overlap with the surrounding workflow too. “Everything outside the editor is ours” would also be too broad.
- Framewright's evidence is its own completed films, retained defects/corrections, accepted scopes, package recovery and combinations of tools. Those are inspectable case evidence, not proof of a moat or automatic creative quality.
- The strongest near-term question is whether a creator or partner can get a coherent film and a dependable later correction with less repeated work. Active time, revision rounds, cost and help required must be measured before claiming savings.
- Keep author intent, creative acceptance, source/rights checks, provider charges and actual publication distinct. A rendered file or an agent's self-check is not audience validation.

Do not narrow all creative production to screencasts: they are the best immediate comparison material, not the studio's entire future scope. Preserve partner-facing tool tests and commissioned/creative films in the adopted audience map. [Existing vision](../vision.md), [business operations](../studio-production-management.md).

### EDITROOM-01 — correction and saved project

Question: can a small native editor handle a real Framewright correction more conveniently than the retained route?

30min preparation limit after owner selection. Check signed release/version, external install/data paths and free export first. No new neural weights, paid requests, subscription, agent-account change or source upload. Use manual editing/existing-agent route; do not activate optional Local Magic or semantic indexing merely to complete setup. If mandatory model downloads or internal storage requirements violate current constraints, stop as blocked with the exact reason.

Import three shots and existing audio; add a readable label, one restrained grade and one speed change. Save, quit, reopen, correct only the label and export MP4. Check duration/frames/aspect, A/V sync, title legibility, retained timing/grade, project persistence and undo. Record actual setup/active-edit time and missing portability features. Do not infer NLE interchange from “export any shape”.

### DAYDREAM-01 — agent correction and handoff

Question: does its visible timeline + existing agent improve a precise revision and preserve a usable handoff?

30min preparation limit after owner selection. Verify the free plan and remaining account quotas first; target at most **20 MCP calls** and record actual usage. No paid upgrade, automatic overage, new weights or generation. Avoid transcription/search if not needed. Verify which project context the connected provider receives; local-footage marketing is not a complete data-flow audit. Connect only the scoped integration if selected.

Build the same12s brief; change one label/one shot boundary, undo and redo, save/quit/reopen and export MP4 plus the documented interchange file. Inspect saved project and media references; attempt isolated-copy reopening only if supported. Parse/inspect XML and report lost features; do not call this a Resolve/FCP round trip without actually reopening there. Free-tier saved-recipe restrictions must not be bypassed or described as tested.

### Keep/drop rule

Score output quality and edit accuracy first, then recovery, operator effort, integration friction, extra costs and portability. Retain an additional tool only if it solves a named production gap better enough to justify maintenance. A setup failure is not a quality failure; a free export is not proof of long-form reliability. If neither improves the matched task, keep the current route and close both probes without expanding the tool set.
