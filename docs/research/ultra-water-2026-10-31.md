# Ultra-Water —31October2026

Status: assessed, queued ULTRA-WATER-01; not installed or benchmarked locally.
Maps to M4/R05 (controlled browser scenes), optional R07 reuse only for a motivated
water shot. It does not interrupt the current Good Dog correction.

Sources: [repository](https://github.com/Humpalumps/Ultra-Water),
[demo](https://gelatin.games/ultrawater),
[package](https://github.com/Humpalumps/Ultra-Water/blob/master/package.json),
[notices](https://github.com/Humpalumps/Ultra-Water/blob/master/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
Repository describes a Three.js/WebGL2 FFT ocean with foam, shore breaking,
caustics, underwater views and floating props. MIT with preserved third-party
notices. Dependencies include Three.js0.186.1+, lil-gui and Vite.

Published200+fps figures are author measurements on an RTX3060 at a stated
resolution/settings; they are not Mac evidence. Mobile profiles are also
documented, but neither proves export suitability or deterministic seeking here.
The demo could not be retrieved by the research browser; no live visual judgement
or local run is claimed. A separate elements/spells collection was mentioned by
the owner without a link; do not attribute it to this checkout without evidence.

## First bounded test

One isolated pinned checkout, one job at a time on this Mac. Start at720p with
reduced quality:5–8s fixed camera→one parameter change→repeat capture. Save
revision, prerequisites, settings and resource measurements. Check camera/wave
continuity, foam, object contact, stable frame pacing and whether absolute-time
replay needs simulation reset/pre-roll. Compare against an existing simple-water
scene for quality and render cost. Verify UI-free recording and a complete decode;
visual acceptance remains a human decision. Stop on GPU pressure, unstable
playback or an unsupported replay/export path. No paid resources, game shell,
whole-engine adoption or simulation-of-any-liquid claim.

Potential uses: coastal establishing shot, flooded street detail, water transition
or a short stylised insert. It is not yet validated for a small pouring drop,
fluid containers or physically accurate contact with arbitrary geometry. Codex
can modify selected scene/effect code after inspection; that is an adaptation
plan, not a tested integration.
