## What should remain outside an immediate course promise

- Natural character acting,autonomous cinematic film production and Quixote:rejected/limited evidence and explicit freeze. Do not reopen them or advertise solved continuity.
- A fully autonomous end-to-end creative studio:individual bounded successes and owner corrections do not establish universal unattended production.
- Financial trading/product mastery:the Web3 portfolio is explanatory,with product/testing/legal limits. It is useful communication evidence,not trader qualification.
- Art/media/film compilations whose reusable third-party images,music or footage have unresolved teaching rights. A public social upload is not a new course-use grant.
