## Trial method and reuse

1. Pin one cleared reference and action prompt; request one short output, stable
camera, no audio or automatic prompt enhancement for the comparator.
2. Confirm model-specific quote and current account balance before submission;
retain submitted state and avoid retrying an ambiguous submission.
3. Download actual media through the browser, probe resolution/fps/duration/audio,
decode fully and inspect start/middle/end plus movement in a playable comparison.
Requested duration/resolution can differ from the file.
4. Keep provider terms, actual allowance, technical checks, owner preference,
publication clearance and production continuity as independent evidence.
5. Return only an accepted useful shot to the film; a catalogue trial is not
permission to assemble the full three-minute film ahead of its pilot checkpoint.

Private prompt, inputs, files, source hashes and account observations remain with
the local Good Dog production. No account identity or media is committed here.
