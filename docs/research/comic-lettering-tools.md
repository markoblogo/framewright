## Applied lettering and colour recipe

[Blambot guidance](https://blambot.com/pages/comic-book-grammar-tradition) distinguishes ordinary, shout, thought and off-panel speech; speech tails should aim toward the mouth and stop before the face. [Anime Ace](https://blambot.com/products/anime-ace-2) is an explicit manga-labelled reference in the publisher’s catalogue, not evidence of one universal Japanese-animation font. It was not downloaded or licensed for this film.

The current local candidate instead uses retained SIL-OFL [Kalam](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/kalam) Latin capitals for dialogue and [Comic Neue](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/comicneue) for narrative/SFX cards. Elliptical speech, irregular shout outlines,3x antialiasing, individually assigned mouth targets, generous inset and actor-safe positions replace generic bottom rectangles. The off-panel Mercer answer uses an edge-directed tail; it is not attributed to an on-screen family member. These overlays are our existing local compositor, **not output from the newly tested editor**.

Small cyan water highlights, amber phone/witness cues and crimson braking supplement the previously accepted yellow impact/red ending. Owner reported the ending red was hard to notice: the final narrative card covered the feather tip. Revised staging moves the card to upper-right space and strengthens existing red marks. Total170s, no voice, selected B score and quiet white ending retained. Private recipes, fonts/licences, exported demo PNG, screenshot, MP4 hashes and per-scene checks stay in the external production package. Neither new balloon styling nor colour changes are owner-accepted yet.

## Promotion gates

Use verified native PNG/SVG output, transparent alpha, readable text inset, source-linked tails, correct on/offscreen speaker, actor-safe placement, deterministic timing, portrait restaging and retained licence/provenance as separate checks. A static PNG pass does not establish animated tracking or video integration. Follow up with one chosen tool only, without broadening the current film’s scope.
