# Video-generation challenges lesson — production-method intake

2 November 2026. Owner-supplied Russian lesson, `УРОК_Вызовы_видеогенерации_2026_v3_720p.mp4`,
and timestamped chapter summary. Research and sampled inspection, not a new
production, provider test or general fact-check of the entire lecture.

## What was actually inspected

Local probe: 3648.7s (60:48.7), H.264 1280×720, AAC, no subtitle stream.
Twelve sampled frames around the supplied chapters show the structured-prompt
assistant, multimodal references, a motion skeleton, shot-reference catalogue,
generation rejection counts, fragmented director tools, character drift and the
five-stage location/frame/skeleton/generation/edit pipeline. Chapter headings are
navigation supplied by the owner, not exact scene boundaries. Full audiovisual
viewing/listening was not performed. Private source hash, sample times and contact
sheet are retained under `records/research-2026-11-02-video-lesson/` on external
studio storage; no source film or extracted imagery is committed.

Four 45s audio excerpts were prepared for local ASR with the installed Whisper
small CPU model. The first excerpt had not produced a usable transcript after
about five minutes; observed CPU usage was about 622% and RSS about 970 MiB.
The owned process was stopped, without retry, install or model download. Thus no
claim below rests on an automatically obtained complete transcript. This is a
negative resource observation for this invocation, not a diagnosis of Whisper or
all local ASR. Prefer the supplied summary/available captions for this intake;
select a cheaper already-available transcription path before another long lesson.

**Sequence:** agree the scene and references → cheap local previz → choose one
primary learning objective → confirm current provider/access/rights and attempt
cap → compare in context → owner review → retain the selected result and concise
failure evidence. Do not run the two new subtrials on every film or start them now.
MOTION-GUIDE-01 complements the earlier reverse-direction
[generated-video → editable rig proposal](video-guided-rig-animation.md); it does
not prove either direction works. Existing local Blender and direct video edits
remain the fallbacks if no free/already-paid endpoint supports the required input.

## Facts to keep separate from the author's account

- **Version-specific references:** ByteDance's [Seedance 2.0 announcement](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/official-launch-of-seedance-2-0) states up to 9 image / 3 video / 3 audio references and 15s output. The [2.5 announcement](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5) states up to 30 / 10 / 10, up to 30s, clay/motion references and timestamp-oriented editing. Vendor capability statements are not local/account availability, quality or free entitlement. Do not assign the larger numbers to 2.0 or every reseller UI.
- **Veo control:** [DeepMind's product page](https://deepmind.google/models/veo/) describes references, camera controls and first/last-frame guidance. It does not establish access to every feature through the owner's current Flow/AI Studio plan or guarantee that unrelated scene content survives a change.
- **Sora status:** [OpenAI's discontinuation notice](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation) dates web/app discontinuation to 26 April 2026 and API discontinuation to 24 September 2026. Retain this as an availability refresh before proposing any legacy Sora route. The notice does not establish the lesson's margin, download, engagement or business-cause claims.
- **Cost and energy:** the lecture's revenue, per-second pricing, acceptance ratios, personal spend and microwave comparison are source-author reports/examples. No underlying invoices, energy measurement boundary or comparable current account quote was audited here. They are not Framewright budget estimates or measured savings; model prices and free credits are refreshed at actual use.
- **Provenance:** preserve sources, transformations and applicable provider/platform disclosure. [C2PA's explainer](https://c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.2/explainer/Explainer.html) distinguishes verifiable provenance from truth judgments. Missing credentials do not prove real footage; a valid credential does not prove the depicted event occurred. The lecture's four-layer legal framing is not adopted as a universal jurisdiction checklist. No watermark removal or detector-evasion trial is queued.
- **Other claims:** industry rankings, AGI/physics explanations, contests/prizes, speech-error rates, financial results and 90,000-publication distribution claims were not independently established. No auto-publication or mass distribution follows from this intake.

## Disposition

High relevance as a workflow/limitation reference, not a new renderer or required
paid bundle. Add only MOTION-GUIDE-01 and V2V-PRESERVE-01 as conditional bounded
subtrials; enrich existing PARAM-SHOT-01, PREVIS-01, COST-01 and OM-BOARD-01 instead
of duplicate systems. Provider candidates keep their existing statuses. No changed
production priority, budget, account grant, upload, provider generation or publication.
