## Source and claim check

- [Original project](https://bosnadev.com/lab/riemann/app): browser page fetch
  blocked; owner's in-app screen recording provides the visual evidence. We
  do not claim the explorer performs formal verification or that its map
  constitutes the proof, or assert its authorship or scientific effectiveness.
- [Alpöge's own announcement](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf), 20 November: author
  reports a proof developed with Claude, released as a Lean formalization;
  he describes ongoing understanding/simplification. No independent claim of
  novel proof or unsupervised AI discovery.
- [Lean FRO / Mathlib joint statement](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/),
  21 November: certifies correctness of statement and proof; reproducible build,
  exact Mathlib target comparison, multiple kernel checks of inert export.
  Accordingly film says verified formal proof, not merely unverified claim.
- [Michael Rubinstein's Waterloo verification and exposition](https://math.uwaterloo.ca/~mrubinst/alpoge_claude_grh/):
  175,187 Lean files, about 17 million lines; finished independent checks and
  human-readable exposition still a working draft. The exact 175,187 refers
  to his count of the released archive, not exclusively AI-authored lines.
- [Clay problem definition](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/riemann-hypothesis/):
  background only; no claim regarding prize or institutional closure.

Editorial analogy: AI-created business software may outgrow routine manual
review; verification checks *specified* properties, it does not establish
that specifications capture real human needs. No specific future prediction
or guarantee that Lean process transfers directly to every business system.
