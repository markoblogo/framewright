## First — Audio-Driven Avatars: Lip Sync Is Only One Part of the Test

Original menu candidate10. Updated source: [Kandinsky S2V report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19290), [official project/demos](https://kandinskyavatar.github.io/) and [repository](https://github.com/kandinskylab/kandinsky-s2v), checked8November. The paper describes image+speech+prompt-driven animation at25fps, with Lite2B/Pro21B variants and author-reported comparisons. These are upstream claims, not our model measurements or proof of Mac feasibility.

Proposed90–110s English, native YouTube16:9/TikTok9:16. Hook: a mouth can match every syllable while the person still looks wrong. Explain four coupled channels: lips, expression, head/body pose, gestures. Pair labelled official examples, subject to source-use clearance, with original authored motion/diagnostic overlays. The M01 experience motivates the question; it is not a Kandinsky test. No inference, new weights or paid video generation required for the proposed explanatory route.

Chosen because it offers strong moving visuals, a concrete production question and direct transferable learning from M01. Cost/effort should stay bounded with existing render/audio tools. Donor export, usage terms, actual whole-motion inspection and source labels remain gates before assembly. If suitable donor reuse fails, use original diagrams or defer, not download an unadmitted model to rescue the concept. Creative/listening acceptance remains separate. No forecast of reach/revenue.

## Reserve — Free Local Coding Agents: Check Junie's Hardware Requirements First

Documentation currently lists macOS26+, AppleSiliconM4+,48GBRAM and~21GBfree, or Windows11/NVIDIA24GBVRAM. Installer/older release surfaces show differing memory/chip thresholds: pin the displayed version/date and distinguish documented support from installer implementation; never present one timeless universal requirement.

Proposed80–100s English in both aspects: hardware decision map → model/runtime separation → download/storage/context costs → what we have and have not tested. No installation, model download, token-speed benchmark or account setting needed. Useful low-production-cost fallback with source screenshots and large original animated decision cards. Prior to script, recheck the exact pinned requirements and avoid claiming our host runs it.
