## What was produced

An original modular3D café street moves through daylight, golden hour and night, with cars, walking figures, plants, café steam and camera movement. Geometry, materials, texture signs, typography and animation are authored locally with retained Three.js180/Chrome/Playwright; FFmpeg encodes and finishes the sound. Both aspects share the original scene and narration but have independent camera framing and text placement. The miniature is fictional; this is an artistic lighting essay, not a controlled study of viewer emotions or production speed.

A100-word English Fola take was generated once in Google AI Studio's normal tablet-browser UI. The actual45.92-second PCM24kHz mono WAV was downloaded on Android, transferred through authorized ADB and verified by matching device/Mac SHA256 plus full decode. Six silence insertions place narration on the56-second timeline without changing speech speed/pitch. Original deterministic procedural music and street effects supply the soft bed; no third-party samples or models were used. The retained runtime includes Three.js's MIT notice.

Each package contains a native MP4, matching coverJPEG, English title/caption/description/tags/first comment, transcript, SRT/VTT, credits, alt text and hash manifest. Both packages also have download ZIPs and one range-enabled review page. Full sources, stems and recipe remain on Work; no binary or copied runtime is added to Git.
