## Method and retained evidence

The [interactive artifact](https://claude.ai/artifact/CTjrZXVdUgKJ6qJXrvvr28) was captured in Android Chrome in landscape and portrait. Seven selections pass a blue gate, city panorama, riverside, temple tower, streets, rooftops and night view. Heavy browser rendering produced roughly7–12fps. Mac FFmpeg selected/cropped/interpolated the initial montage; native InShot reset canvas, adjusted colour, added animated entry/end titles and mixed original locally synthesised ambient music. Do not attribute preprocessing to InShot or claim its1080p export creates new detail from the prepared720p source. No narration.

Both MP4 files fully decoded; sampled encoded titles/layouts and loopback playback/forward-backward seeking were checked. Native output adds two endpoint frames; the vertical null-muxer emits an endpoint-rounding warning although presentation timestamps strictly increase. Frame-exact parity and transferable editable projects remain unqualified. Owner picture/sound acceptance is separate from those technical limits.

Private studio `productions/babylon-virtual-tour/` retains source captures, preprocessing and original music generators, native accepted masters, hashes/QA in `receipt.json`, and publication packages under `review/packages/`. Package video paths are same-volume hard links to accepted bytes. Covers are encoded-frame selections at1.5s, independently checked at native/reduced size; cover/text acceptance is not inherited from film acceptance. Optional SRT files label ambient music only, with no fabricated spoken transcript. `edit/package.py` reproduces package text/cover selection. Native editable drafts stay on the tablet.
