## Source and creative decisions

no Junie installation, model download, inference or benchmark was performed.

The documented Mac route lists macOS26+, M4+, 48GB RAM or more; Windows lists
Windows11, discrete NVIDIA RTX Ampere+, 24GB VRAM or more. Both list about21GB free
disk. The pinned shell RAM guard uses40GiB minimum/60GiB recommended; that is not a
performance guarantee or a replacement for the published support requirements.
Additional platform/runtime details remain in the linked documentation. The16GB
example concerns this Mac route, not every local model; local inference alone
does not establish an entirely offline tool workflow.

The owner accepted the English script but requested a stronger picture than diagrams
alone. An original lavender/lime hardware keyframe was accepted, followed by both

## Implementation and limits

- Local Blender4.5.15 LTS Eevee scene: camera orbit, moving data blocks and expanding
  conceptual interface/engine/model layers. No real Junie UI or measured workload.
- 720p Blender/pilot material is enlarged for1080p delivery alongside native1080p
  Pillow/FFmpeg graphics. Source12fps, pilot frame blending and final24fps are
  explicit quality limits, not recovered high-resolution detail or general tool qualification.
- English Supertonic-3 M1 narration and a quiet original local electronic bed;
  the two formats have matching decoded audio. Shared SRT uses approximate phrase timing.
- Full decode, timing/dimensions, sampled layouts, levels and native two-way seeking
  passed. Owner listening/creative acceptance remains a separate journal observation.
- Separate original covers/copy pages were prepared. The first portrait cover clipped
  its headline in a simulated square crop; a second composition fixed it. Actual
  platform-grid crop and uploaded artwork are not independently observed.
