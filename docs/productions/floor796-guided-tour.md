## Reusable approach

- **Observe and record:** explore [Floor796](https://floor796.com/) and verify identities against the site's own labels where needed. The user recorded 4:47 of the actual browser experience; the desktop and browser chrome were excluded from outputs. Avoid invented character encounters or turning an exploratory tour into a checklist of secrets.
- **Edit for each screen:** choose nine continuous excerpts, allowing time to inspect scenes. Reframe each passage separately for 16:9 and 9:16; check subjects against the commentary. The vertical robot and electrical-scene crops needed manual adjustment. The source is an animated isometric work: enlargement does not create additional detail.
- **Narration and sound:** brief context and sparse, expressive female English observations; owner accepted the Fola sample, then both complete films. Owner supplied “Ghost in the Machine” and requested continuous background music at 25–30% of source level under voice at source level; the mix uses 27%, no sidechain ducking, with a peak limiter.
- **Finish and packaging:** a restrained increase in contrast and saturation follows owner feedback. Exported accepted versions are H.264/AAC, 1280×720 and 720×1280, each 2:24. Film-frame covers and separate English SRT files were prepared. The SRT follows the editing spans; word-level timing/transcription was not independently verified.

The owner reports a previous [Floor796 screencast](https://youtu.be/WFui7ufUyJs) has remained on YouTube for over a year without problems. That is useful owner-reported history, not a verified current rights or monetisation determination. Permission/licence for this third-party world and rights for the exact music/voice use were not independently established. No engagement, growth, income, cost or savings outcome is asserted.

## Retention and limits

Private studio `productions/floor796-guided-tour/` retains the owner screencast, voice takes, a copy of the requested music, editable render/mix recipes, both accepted masters and publication packages. Rendered clip intermediates and assembled picture-only exports can be regenerated from the retained source and recipe; their removal does not remove the accepted films. The original user desktop capture is retained separately. The project is not a general-purpose interactive-world recorder or a guarantee of automatic character identification, subtitle accuracy or acceptable reuse terms.
