# Transit Speed — bilingual demo lesson

The owner accepted both v4 masters on 22 November 2026. The receiving project
confirmed receipt of the Ukrainian and Hungarian MP4s and matching SRTs. This
is a private training handoff. The earlier [partner presentation](transit-speed-private-pitch.md)
is a separate production.

## Reusable method

Capture the product in a synthetic-data demo. Use static context images with
camera moves, and a short 3D cutaway to explain a stock transfer. Mask device
inserts to the screen shape. Enlarge workflow evidence where the viewer needs
to read it. Use a centred opening title and closing destination and credit.

Cut the 17 scenes against both narrations on a single picture timeline. Remove
music-only static holds and retime the voice takes gently with pitch preserved.
Time each subtitle file against its final narration. The v4 review cut ran
5:58, down from 8:40. Keep punctuation, checks, and subtitles specific to each
language while retaining the same product picture.

## Evidence

Screencast composites and 3D context clips were finished with FFmpeg. Local
Supertonic 3 narration was retained for both languages in the accepted edit.
The two 1080p exports have 8,603 matching video frames by stream hash and
different audio streams. The Ukrainian SRT has 61 cues and the Hungarian SRT
has 60. Separate native-speaker language review and public publication are
unverified. The demo recordings, scripts, masters and subtitles remain private.
