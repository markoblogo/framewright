# FW identity — accepted, optional in films

Owner accepted9November2026: **FW only**, a folded silver ribbon on black,
unfolding rather than a generic spin. The selected motion starts from pure black.
It is29frames at30fps, **0.967seconds**, with a thin-metal rustle and light ring.
Both landscape1280×720 and portrait720×1280 masters are retained privately.

The exact accepted raster artwork is mapped to a locally curled3D surface.
This is a texture-based2.5D/3D effect, not physical simulation of one continuous
metal strip. The original procedural sound is synthetic, not a field recording.
The earlier EW-like form and procedural-mesh motion were rejected; do not restore them.

## Adoption experiment remains open

The brand is accepted; routine placement is **not**. Test the short ending and a
small watermark in selected future films, checking readability, safe margins,
colour/background conflicts and whether the ending feels intrusive in both formats.
Watermark positioning is platform-specific; lower-right can conflict with controls.
No automatic insertion. A flat transparent watermark has not yet been delivered.
Keep the matched two-topic [centre-crop experiment](../experiment-queue.md#centre-crop-01--next-two-radar-films-plus-an-experiment-film9november2026)
separate from brand-placement acceptance. No licence or telemetry change.
