# The Crop Missed the Action

The owner accepted the color-graded 31.5-second landscape edit and supplied
the [YouTube URL](https://youtu.be/OQm-MV2m0_8) on 23 November 2026
(`owner_provided_url`). YouTube oEmbed returned “How to Reframe Horizontal
Video for 9:16 in FFmpeg” and “ABV — AI · Books · Validation”. The live
visibility setting, metadata beyond oEmbed and uploaded media identity were
not independently checked. The publication package reuses the accepted
1280×720 master and includes a cover and separate English subtitles.

## One frame, two crop positions

Both FFmpeg versions use the same 75-frame input, 396×704 crop window and
720×1280 output. The fixed-center window begins at x=442. The manual window
moves left with the bucket and stops at the source edge. On input frame 64,
2.133 seconds into the excerpt, the center crop loses the bucket. The moving
window retains the bucket and the start of the discharge. The comparison
does not assess where the water lands or whether the entire plume fits.

The owner asked for stronger contrast and saturation. The final cut applies
one color correction before either crop, then shows the wide shot, each
crop's position and synchronized views. It uses archival
[Washington Air National Guard footage by MSgt Brandy Burke](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/1015999/wa-national-guard-provides-aviation-support-modrite-wildfire-response).
The footage, geometry, color recipe and strict decode receipt are retained
with the private studio package.

[Official LTX Reframe](https://comfy.org/ltx-vfx-features/) uses a Partner API
to extend the image with generated content. Authorized access and credits
were unconfirmed. No LTX calls were made, and the comparison remains open.
