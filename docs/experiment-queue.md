## Conditional support studies

- **auto-editor:** when a real long-form source needs pause removal. Label pauses,
  protect phrase edges and check sync; do not cut an already-paced 15s promo just
  to exercise the tool.
- **MLX-Audio diarization / pyannote:** authorised multi-speaker source with manual
  turn/overlap labels; exact model terms/token/telemetry first. TTS success does not
  qualify diarization, identity verification or voice cloning.
- **claude-video / Watch:** a retained local clip, bounded sampled frames/transcript
  versus known events. Inspect dependency and cloud-fallback behaviour first;
  sampled evidence does not replace full decode, listening or temporal checks.
- **yt-dlp:** acquisition only when authorised source media lacks a local master.
  **Editly:** only for a concrete assembly gap. **Shipvideo:** cloud/account and
  restricted input review before a future comparison. **LTX-2:** remains deferred
  until exact weight terms, hardware and storage/download budget are resolved.

## Decision and release gates

Before a review/trial, find the canonical tool in the ledger and read its relevant
finding, evidence and rights. After every review, attempt or decision, update it,
including rejection, blocked setup, rights restriction, unknown result or no-op.
Name the changed revision, terms, task or unresolved feature before any repeat.
Never erase a failed attempt when a later workaround succeeds.
