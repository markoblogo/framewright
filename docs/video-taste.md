# Video taste — this studio's reusable owner preferences

Updated 19 November 2026. These rules preserve explicit owner corrections and
standing preferences from the supplied production conversation and linked cases.
They are scoped working instructions, not universal design laws or model qualification.
The current brief and a later explicit owner decision take precedence. Each new
film still needs its own inspections and acceptance; no historical master is changed.

Use [capture and application procedure](../skills/video-production-router/references/video-taste.md).
Select only applicable IDs in the existing production brief; inspect their evidence
before showing the candidate. Read the underlying case only when its scope matters.
Keep canonical voice/brand/method decisions in their owning documents rather than
copying settings into a competing policy. New inferred generalisations remain proposals.

| ID | Applies when | Prevents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VT-MOTION | Sustained character/illustration scenes | A moving pilot followed by a whole film of blinking or pose swaps |
| VT-SCENES | Narrative Radar explainers and business video pitches | A film that remains a static slide deck or only diagrams |
| VT-READ | Text, diagrams and animated drawing over picture | Lines/titles that disappear on bright scenery or a small screen |
| VT-FRAME | Both requested aspect variants / centre crop | Overlays or reframing hide the subject or meaningful action |
| VT-VOICE | New narrated films | Mechanical reserve speech or a short voice probe treated as long-take approval |
| VT-COVER | Publication packaging | Cover treatment detached from the accepted film |
| VT-MIX | Voice-led exploratory tours | Background music competes with speech or needs needless fragment-by-fragment gaps |

## VT-MOTION — sustain the scene, not just the pilot

**Rule:** plan meaningful changes of action, camera, composition or insert across
the whole duration. For a sustained speaking/illustrative character, blinking alone
or switching still poses does not satisfy the requested character movement. When the brief requests a living environment or moving object, animate that content itself; camera drift or a light overlay over an otherwise unchanged scene is insufficient.
**Why / owner evidence:** «на 95 сек надо разнообразить активность»; «не просто
менял позы между кадрами, а двигал головой и руками». The stated problem was
static, repetitive picture. [Gemini](productions/gemini-omni-revise.md) and
[Copilot](productions/copilot-review-sources.md) retain the accepted corrections. The owner later called [One Light](productions/one-light-one-way-home.md) «это просто слайд-шоу» despite liking its images/sound: the same motion lesson had not been applied adequately. The owner subsequently accepted both motion-corrected versions; the failed initial check remains part of the lesson.
[KriptoK](productions/kriptok-same-ticker.md#owner-revisionrepetitionstatic-source-windows-and-clipping) adds another failed full-film check:the owner liked the opening/3D graph but rejected repeated page layouts/three-dot decoration and long static source windows. A shared magazine identity must still give each argument an appropriate illustration and action;pilot approval did not approve these interiors. The owner subsequently accepted both corrected full films;the failed initial check remains part of the lesson.
**Check:** review the action plan and encoded movement through each relevant long
scene in the full film. Look for the named subject/environment action within a continuous shot, not only pixel changes, a dissolve, light sweep or camera motion. A short moving opening does not pass this check.
**Limit:** a deliberately quiet or static beat may serve the agreed story. Do not
force a cut every N seconds, distort whole faces to move a hand, or claim natural
acting from rigid layers. Use the [full-film proposal](creative-proposal.md).

## VT-SCENES — explain with moving scenes as well as diagrams

**Rule:** in these narrative explainers/pitches, include fitting animated imagery,
real footage/screencasts or cinematic inserts alongside diagrams/interface explanation.
The scene should contribute to the point being made; a new tool is not required.
**Why / owner evidence:** «надо чтобы кроме схематики были и анимированные картинки»;
«мы же видео презентацию делаем — надо их оживить». Sources:
[Prisma](productions/prisma-plugin-verify.md) and [Transit Speed](productions/transit-speed-private-pitch.md).
**Check:** inspect the complete scene sequence and actual exports, not only a storyboard
or a moving camera over one unchanged slide. Use actual screen captures where selected.
**Limit:** this is not a requirement to add generated cutaways to a real-world tour,
abstract film or SSI's established receiving-project template. Keep factual demonstrations
and authored interface proposals distinct, as their case records require.

Owner correction19November2026:[curl](productions/curl-maintenance-workshop.md)was formally animated but felt dry and unengaging. Retain useful illustrations while varying the visual evidence and treatment:appropriate genuine site/document recordings,photographic context and sourced video can carry distinct beats. This is an editorial lesson about that repetitive cut,not a requirement to add stock to every film or a claim that movement alone passed creative review.

## VT-READ — inspect the delivered small-screen result

**Rule:** make text and important construction lines readable over their actual
background in the encoded output and reduced viewing size. Adjust weight, colour,
backing or placement where needed; native-resolution inspection alone is insufficient.
**Why / owner evidence:** «едва видно в ютюб версии, а в тикток версии вообще не
видно»; the owner requested thicker, clearly noticeable lines. See the
[Pantheon worked correction](productions/pantheon-chatgpt-orb.md#worked-example-v2-to-v3).
**Check:** inspect representative encoded moments and reduced landscape/portrait
previews, including the brightest/busiest relevant backgrounds. Name the size checked.
**Limit:** the Pantheon's blue 8 px / white 12 px treatment and 335/640 px previews
are that case's solution, not mandatory colours, widths or universal thresholds.

## VT-FRAME — keep the subject and action visible in each format

**Rule:** place overlays and reframe each requested output so they do not obscure
the relevant body, object or action. For ordinary footage reframing, enlarge proportionally and crop to fill; do not infer nonuniform deformation from “stretch” or “fill the screen”. Review the actual vertical result even if it
is a crop of a landscape master.
**Why / owner evidence:** «в тикток плашки закрывают нижнюю половину тела робота»;
the owner requested moving the robot up. [Gemini](productions/gemini-omni-revise.md),
[Copilot](productions/copilot-review-sources.md) and [Floor796](productions/floor796-guided-tour.md)
record overlay/crop decisions. In [the English clip localisation](productions/yapfiles-3797572-localization.md), the owner corrected the agent’s anamorphic full-frame attempt: “не деформировать а масштабировать”.
**Check:** inspect encoded framing at important action moments in both requested
variants; a correct landscape view does not establish a usable portrait crop. Inspect familiar round/rectangular objects and the scaling settings for unintended deformation.
**Limit:** both [independent layouts and centre-safe cropping](methods/centre-safe-dual-format.md)
are accepted techniques. Do not force a second native composition if the selected
crop works, or insist on showing the whole body in an intentional close-up.

Owner correction20November2026:[BuyMore](productions/buymore-problems-delivered.md#owner-correction-smartphone-portraitv2)accepted the desktop film but returned its rotated-monitor portrait treatment. For this shopping-sketch adaptation,the intended mobile device is a smartphone with touch conventions and no mouse pointer;native aspect geometry alone did not establish a believable device choice. Owner accepted smartphonev2. This does not prohibit portrait monitors in other contexts.

**Player check:** compare the actual encoded dimensions with the HTML video box in both aspects. A portrait MP4 inside a width-forced,height-capped landscape box can show misleading side fields. Remove unintended player/section framing before judging the export. Owner evidence17November:[Agent402](productions/agent402-tabletop.md).

Owner correction19November2026:[curl](productions/curl-maintenance-workshop.md)had low/off-centre3D stages,heading/source-panel collisions and important geometry hidden by native player controls. Evaluate the complete text/illustration/source hierarchy in both aspects,including open player controls;geometry fitting without the final player's occlusion is insufficient. Preserve explicit safe regions for the current delivery,inspect the encoded shot boundaries and revise contradictory placements. Numerical overlap checks remain supporting evidence,not visual acceptance.

## VT-VOICE — natural performance, scoped voice choices

**Rule:** follow [voice selection](voice-selection.md): natural expressive narration
where an eligible route is available; stock Supertonic stays reserve-only. Ukrainian
uses the [Fola preference](ukrainian-voice-baseline.md) until superseded. A short
probe does not approve the pronunciation, pace or acting of a whole long take.
**Why / owner evidence:** «слишком “робот”»; the owner also rejected the long
Ukrainian take's slow delivery and stress errors after liking its short pilot.
**Check:** retain the chosen take and evaluate the full performance in context;
record listening and owner acceptance separately from decode/level checks.
**Limit:** no global Knox default, automatic voice substitution, paid call or changed
SSI worker follows. Character casting and Riko's rough delivery remain case choices.

## VT-COVER — keep publication art connected to the film

**Rule:** default to the film's palette and visual treatment for its cover, with
readable short copy in the video's language and the subject visible. Inspect native
and thumbnail sizes under the [packaging preference](production-guide.md#owner-workspace-packaging-preference).
**Why / owner evidence:** «обложку стоит сделать в цветах ролика» during
[Premation](productions/premation-sync-test.md). Palette preference is explicit;
no additional reason or claim of better click-through was supplied.
**Check:** compare each requested cover with the accepted film at full/reduced size;
film acceptance alone is not a separate cover decision.
**Limit:** an explicitly selected different cover treatment or text-free image
supersedes this default; branding placement is governed by the selected brief.

**Brand check:** if an FW ident is selected,use the [accepted silver-ribbon masters](productions/fw-identity.md),with matching aspect and verified source. Do not substitute a freshly typed FW card. Owner explicitly required the canonical ident17November;this is not a requirement to brand every film.

**Check the cut across the full voiceover.** For [Half a Life by 18?](productions/half-a-life-by-18.md#owner-correction-and-picture-edit-v2), the owner liked the individual animated charts and narration but returned the full edit for long holds and a lack of human feeling. Preserve the graphics at the explanatory beats and use real subject action to carry the emotional beats. Check both crops and the unchanged narration timeline.

## VT-MIX — soft continuous music under tour commentary

**Rule:** for a voice-led exploratory tour, start with clear foreground speech and
a soft continuous bed. The owner's reference is voice 100%, music about 25–30% on
an editor's volume controls; this is a starting balance, not a loudness standard.
**Why / owner evidence:** «музыка не перебивает голос и в паузах негромко слышна».
[Floor796](productions/floor796-guided-tour.md) retains this preference and mix.
**Check:** listen to speech and pauses with the actual chosen sources; adjust if
intelligibility fails. A percentage alone does not prove the audible balance.
**Limit:** source loudness and editor controls differ. This does not require music
throughout RUN//SIGNAL, change a music-led film, prohibit useful ducking or replace
actual listening with gain arithmetic.

## Keep case-only details in their cases

The phone-screen tracking repair and [Same Street](productions/same-street-three-moods.md)'s wheel/foot surface contact are functional corrections, not new taste laws. Surface-height, planted-stance and encoded contact inspections live in Street's recipe; the requested20% tempo increase stays case-specific.
The Pantheon line widths, Vaybolet's rough male voice, Floor796's female reactions,
Transit Speed's AMI ending and the rejected frame-filling Page Flip use stay scoped.
[FW](productions/fw-identity.md) is optional and its accepted sting is under one second;
this preference work does not mandate a watermark/ending in every film.
A new palette, fixed cut frequency or universal character style needs its own owner
instruction. Record any actual conflict/exception in the affected film, and update
this index only at the authorised scope.
