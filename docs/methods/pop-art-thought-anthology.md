# Pop-art thought anthology in two formats

Repeatable R04/R05/R07/R09 / M3–M5 craft route from [Marcus Aurelius vs Zombies](../productions/marcus-zombie-thoughts.md), 7 November 2026. One owner-reviewed production, not general character, contact, voice or provider qualification.

## Repeatable recipe

1. **Lock the source, audience and visual rules.** Read the book and select five self-contained ideas before generating. Agree the shared opening, characteristic speaker and ending. Preserve supplied illustration identity, engraved black halftone and magenta/cyan/yellow palette. Here the Roman emperor/legionaries coexist with office zombies and a modern city; the staging and dialogue are a contemporary fictional adaptation, not historical quotations. Keep the later owner decision authoritative: one complete film per aspect superseded five standalone TikToks.
2. **Build each idea as action plus conclusion.** One useful choice becomes one short visible action, followed by the emperor's sentence. Approve stills, then short moving pilots. Keep provider originals, exact job IDs, prompts and source hashes; inspect hands, props, ground contact and the encoded cut. Do not resubmit an apparently slow job without checking its existing ID. This case mixed Flow, PixVerse, Vidu and PlayMCP donors; availability and queues varied. No universal free-quota or speed claim follows.
3. **Spend correction effort where it changes perception.** A megaphone or a back-turned speaker avoids demanding lip synchronisation. Put PING/PAUSE in the physical billboard plane with a measured homography; this assumes the retained locked camera, not general tracking. Broad image-space finger deformation failed because the face/phone moved. Three separate masked hand poses, accelerated to 0.4s, were accepted as an implied tap; they are limited animation, not reconstructed hand mechanics. Use an authored messenger for precise typing, deletion and the changed reply.
4. **Keep one accepted sound edit; compose each aspect deliberately.** Approve the commanding synthetic emperor voice before reuse. Local Qwen3-TTS 0.6B/MLX cloning of the fictional reference was accepted as imperfect but usable; no real actor was cloned and general voice fidelity was not measured. Keep generic CC0 city traffic continuous under pings and speech, reduce rather than mute it, and add local water/ceramic cues. Portrait uses directed crops and a native messenger layout; help, dishes and finale received native portrait takes. Copy the exact accepted landscape AAC stream into the portrait mux. The silent ending uses three separated lamp dips, recovery capped at source brightness, then picture/sound fade; no rapid strobe.
5. **Freeze, package and retire carefully.** Review each full format with sound. Check full decode, aspect/SAR/frame rate, duration, final black, phrase timings and byte-range forward/backward seeking. Keep corrected SRT/VTT and editable copy documents with direct downloads, native covers and cover provenance. Bind owner publication URLs to retained master hashes without inferring uploaded-file identity, visibility, revenue or audience results. Prune only a hashed explicit dependency-audited list; keep finals, source donors, accepted module inputs, recipes and failures. Do not overwrite accepted masters merely to prove a rebuild during closeout.

## With and without Framewright

With Framewright: retain the project journal and exact-version acceptance, source/provider receipts, assembly manifest, caption and delivery receipts, publication observations and dependency-aware cleanup records. Use the standard range-enabled preview server. Keep private book, media, models and environments outside Git on the external studio.

Without Framewright: keep the source brief, five action/conclusion cards, character reference, provider job ledger, editable compositions, audio placement sheet, selected sources and hashes, acceptance notes, delivery files and cleanup list in one folder. A compositor plus FFmpeg and a byte-range-capable local server can implement the same workflow. This written alternative has not been independently reproduced.

## Limits and useful next checks

- Character/clothing/hand/prop drift persists across generators; native portrait scenes are alternative takes, not geometrically identical reconstructions. Full-film owner acceptance is the available creative evidence.
- Source resolution varies, including a 540p crowd donor. Scaling into a 720p frame does not recover lost detail; no super-resolution quality qualification was established.
- Crops must prioritise the current action. Native composition costs a fresh take when two actors, hands and a prop cannot fit together. Do not default to repeated panels or blurred side fills.
- Voice cloning, synthetic pings and continuous ambience were accepted for this fictional character. Speech timing and tone still require listening; model output and automated captions are not self-validating. ASR misheard Guard and stretched a word across silence; captions were corrected to the approved script/voice placement.
- Provider account/export/commercial entitlements remain separate from publication and creative acceptance. Existing credits were used; no defensible whole-film savings or time benchmark was recorded. Long provider queues are not local render time.
- On a fitting future production only: compare one difficult interaction in two services using the same acceptance criteria; measure active correction time separately from queue/render time; compare a directed crop against a native portrait take. These are proposed checks, not newly started experiments.
