## Acceptance checklist and limits

- Check recognisability across each join, not only the character sheet. Generated facades,
  anatomy and props drift; a good take is not a stable rig or repeatable identity guarantee.
- A crop is an editorial correction, not a repaired underlying generation. It can hide
  extra subjects or cut needed action. Inspect entry/exit poses, contact, edges and scale.
- Fit bubble ink with padding, source-directed tail and sufficient reading time. Pixel
  bounds alone do not establish comprehension; test small display and platform overlays.
- Portrait simultaneous context/detail panels deliberately repeat the same instant.
  They preserve geography but can feel redundant; no claim of independently animated panels.
-720p sources remain720p information.1080×1920 delivery uses1.5x resampling of the portrait
  working canvas; it is not native1080 generation or recovered detail.
- Family uses still-camera/speaker framing; no full family animation. Selected continuity,
  contact and corrections are accepted in this film, not general motion-model qualification.
- Exact decode, packet identity and white-tail checks are technical evidence. Owner
  creative/listening acceptance, rights, publication and audience results remain separate.

## With and without Framewright

With Framewright, use the brief/revision/review conventions and retained evidence links;
this particular film still uses manually operated recipes rather than claiming admitted
whole-film execution. Without Framewright, keep the same reference sheet, shot table,
source manifest, editor/compositor files, sound stems and version-specific review sheet.
Both routes need an explicit selected final and a resume note listing dependencies,
protected choices and waits. A written alternative is not independent reproduction.
