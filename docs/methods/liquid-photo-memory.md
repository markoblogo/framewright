# Liquid photo memory — selected local recipe

Scope: the owner-accepted54s Gallery Lava film, [production/evidence](../productions/gallery-lava.md). This is a reusable composition method, not qualification of arbitrary photographs, physical-fluid simulation or an upstream WebGL/WebGPU runtime.

## Inputs and mechanism

Use preserved real photographs with a defined order, a small palette and one continuous global time. A background photograph handoff is independent of a foreground carrier field. Photo texture in that field follows a delayed schedule, so previous-image fragments survive into the next scene. Changing a transition mask alone was rejected as a conventional wipe.

Selected controls from this production:

| Control | Observed setting | Purpose / limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carrier | Four merging analytic islands; continuous clock, smooth X/Y movement | Avoid shape resets at photo boundaries. Art-directed fields, not physical wax simulation. |
| Texture memory |4s delayed photo schedule;3.4s background blend | Distinguishable old-image texture inside the incoming picture; strong delays can create muddy double exposure. |
| Real liquid | Cropped wax footage, green-channel key, recoloured teal/amber | Gives irregular natural motion. Assess source licence before integrating a stock donor; attribution alone does not settle ShareAlike. |
| Sampling | Mirror-padded Pillow mesh;320×180 fields;1280×720 output | Avoid black exposed edges and full-film frame caches. Check boundary reflection and very fine detail. |
| Grade | Saturation1.20; endpoint-preserving contrast curve;gamma0.90 | Owner preferred brighter/richer image; not a universal preset for burned-in highlights. |
| Title |126px Shrikhand, two-line visible-glyph centring, RGB255/230/0 | Readable first; then smooth displacement and increasing blur. OFL font/source retained privately. |
| Ending | Travelling refractive blur followed by white fade52.4–53.5s | Dissolve the depicted subject and colour together; retain white hold inside54s. |
| Sound | Original spectral pad/reverse resonances;880Hz sustained ending | A specific owner-approved mix, not a hearing/genre-quality guarantee. |

## Repeatable work sequence

1. Inventory source ownership/attribution and exact files. Choose3donors and a single transition motif; pin the seed/time schedule. Do not generate replacements for real photographs by default.
2. Produce one short motion probe; judge continuity, temporal memory and pacing in motion, with sound. Show two alternatives only when they test a real decision. Here A was accepted; reaction-diffusion B and combined C were not selected.
3. Extend the selected mechanism through a complete colour/composition arc. Source rest points and a final readable object help orientation. Keep text/sound aligned to that arc.
4. Make bounded corrections. Here only the opening6s needed a fresh picture render for v3; copy the approved AAC stream and apply the ending fade during assembly. Retain every input actually read by the selected assembler.
5. Verify whole decode, duration/frame count, exact audio preservation where claimed, typography at full/reduced size, and the encoded ending. Creative acceptance and publication are separate records.

## Limits and next useful trials

- Long blends can conceal movement and lose image contrast; inspect representative textures rather than counting only successful encodes.
- Reduced-size fields and mirror padding may smear detail or repeat an edge. No guarantee of geometric/architectural fidelity or4K detail.
- Reaction-diffusion produced denser patterns and was not selected for this film. It remains a scoped future texture idea, not a failed product or a requirement for the next production.
- A stock film used as a mask/motion input still needs rights analysis. This production retains an unresolved owner decision about ShareAlike; no blanket licence was applied for the owner.
- Future matched timing/quality comparison could test sampling performance; the recorded runs used different implementations/resolutions and do not prove a general speedup.

The transferable method also works without Framewright: keep the same input/time/mask/sound contract in a local compositor, keep a source manifest and versioned final, and obtain human motion/listening review. This written alternative is not a completed independent reproduction.
