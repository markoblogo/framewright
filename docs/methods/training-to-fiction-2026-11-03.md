## What changes in the next working process

1. **Story before effects.** Each shot needs a cause, visible action and consequence.
   Reserve experimental graphics for a document, recollection or change of viewpoint.
   A fluid/ASCII insert cannot substitute for a missing story beat.
2. **Approve donors before montage.** Retain a source-coordinate inventory of moving
   pieces, static areas and occlusions. Test silhouettes/eyes/limbs at their extreme
   poses, plus one neighbouring cut. Pixel equality alone cannot validate anatomy.
3. **Voice before batches.** Rebind one shared external runtime/model manifest; time
   load/read separately from synthesis. Produce a short expressive phrase first.
   A fallback voice needs its own owner decision, rather than silently inheriting F1
   approval. No new installation or cloud spend follows from this recommendation.
4. **Bound revisions.** Keep accepted material fixed, identify the changed interval,
   inspect its incoming/outgoing joins and sound, then retain exact selected hashes.
   Native aspect, group-timed subtitles and approximated geography remain explicit.
5. **Stop repairing an unsuitable mechanism indefinitely.** The poster gait consumed
   repeated corrections. If the method remains unstable, simplify the action, cut
   around it or compare a single alternative; the next trial needs an explicit budget.

## Use with and without Framewright

With Framewright, bind sources/versions and technical/listening/creative decisions
to the existing project journal, use the selected recipe and keep a scoped revision
receipt. Without Framewright, the same method can use a source manifest, shot table,
compositor/editor project, timed voice files and a human review sheet. Neither
written alternative proves independent operator reproduction or general automation.
