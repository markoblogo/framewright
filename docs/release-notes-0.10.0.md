## What changes for the operator

- **Continue the accepted version without obsolete requests.** An explicit
  `supersede-review` relation moves a replaced creative review and its checks to
  history in `brief`, `status` and `blockers`. Original tasks/receipts stay intact.
  The replacement must be completed,current,accepted and cover the original output
  counts/kinds,including the selected final. Selection or publication alone does
  not retire a wait. Drift,rejection,context changes and reopening restore it;
  replacement approval cannot mark the original review done.
- **Retain the creative decisions needed for reuse.** The readable
  [motion specification](../skills/video-production-router/references/motion-spec.md)
  joins intent,action,sound,timing,source and protected elements within the existing
  workflow. It is not an animation parser or second journal. Scoped owner-taste rules
  and the application-indexed arsenal connect tasks/styles/techniques to real cases,
  quality observations and suitable limits rather than promoting every listed tool.
- **Use exact correction/recovery evidence.** [Praxis continuation](experiments/praxis-continuation-2026-11-17.md)
  recovered accepted revision2 and separated one old wait/two pending checks without
  changing media or original records. [Pantheon](productions/pantheon-chatgpt-orb.md#worked-example-v2-to-v3)
  retains a real bounded picture correction with protected sound/later scenes.
  Local preview text/subtitle responses declare UTF-8.
- **Avoid the browser-download dependency for selected English narration.** Local
  Kokoro af_heart full take and two short voice probes have scoped owner acceptance.
  They are alternatives for this prepared Mac route,not Fola equivalence or universal
  voice/language qualification;the optional model/runtime are not bundled.

The source also retains recent film recipes with owner-reported publication links,three AUTOPROD cases accepted

reconciliation. These cases do not establish correction-free autonomy or measured savings.

## Evidence and boundaries

This is a human-and-agent studio workflow increment. Current source remains
`not_evaluated_current_source` for model-routing qualification;historical actor results
are preserved,not relabelled. Software/package tests are not model evaluation,creative
approval,asset clearance,audience growth,revenue or labour savings.

OpenMotion native exports were cancelled without a completed MP4;only its recorded
MCP authoring/revision/package scope is supported. Stable detailed-character acting,
whole-route unattended production,general arbitrary edits and independent clean-machine
operation remain unproven. Portability remains parallel,not a release prerequisite.
Quixote remains paused;SSI,RUN,Udemy,KORG and other selected work keep their own authority.
