## One film, two native packages

1. Read accepted story and exact master. Write audience, viewer payoff, first visual
   event, ending and limits. Preserve author voice, quiet openings where intentional
   and separately accepted narrative. A teaser may have a different opening, clearly
   linked to the whole film; do not silently rewrite the accepted master.
2. YouTube: draft3 distinct truthful title/thumbnail concepts, select one or prepare
   approved native test. Avoid repeating the same information unnecessarily; movie
   name can remain. Inspect cover at full size and feed-size, faces/text/crop/contrast.
   Description first lines explain experience; include accurate credits/book link,
   disclosure/rights, restrained tags. Captions for speech; preserve intentional silence.
3. TikTok: storyboard9:16 instead of stretching16:9. Recompose faces, action, balloon
   tails, text and sound cues; inspect against current device UI overlay. No universal
   hard-coded safe-area guarantee. Firstframe/video hook and profile-grid cover are
   distinct assets. Select/verify cover in actual upload interface. Provide short
   caption with a natural topic phrase, a few relevant hashtags and one optional CTA.
4. QA/publish gate: dimensions/audio/full decode, text reading time and source anchoring,
   mobile view and reduced cover; check actual music/visual licences and declarations,
   account/region/format eligibility. Exact final + exact destination + owner approval
   before posting. No automated deletions, mass comments or credential grants.
5. After publication: retain URL/date/timezone, actual package hashes, any changed
   title/cover, available analytics at comparable24h/7d checkpoints (manual workflow,
   not a new automation). Log next hypothesis; no conclusion from one small sample.

## Measurement that earns a decision

| Surface | Evidence to retain | Interpretation limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube long form | impressions + source, CTR, watch time/average duration, retention graph, subscribers; native A/B verdict if available | Do not infer thumbnail failure from views alone or compare unrelated audiences/ages. Watch-time guardrail protects against empty clickbait. |
| TikTok | average watch time, completion/retention if shown, shares/saves, profile visits, follows; traffic/search evidence if available | Completion depends strongly on length. Replays do not prove preference. Views/follower ratios are contextual, not revenue. No native organic A/B presumed. |
| Business | recorded eligible payout, attributable book-link traffic, confirmed conversions/commissions | UNKNOWN until observed; no invented savings, RPM or sale attribution. |

Keep denominator, reporting window, video age, account, source/export and missingness.
A native test can be inconclusive. Two separate TikTok posts are a sequential editorial
comparison, not a randomised test; avoid near-duplicate spam just to test packaging.
