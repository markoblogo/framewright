## Platform evidence that changes decisions

- [YouTube A/B titles and thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400):
  up to3 alternatives; watch-time outcome; desktop/advanced-feature and content
  eligibility constraints; not Shorts. Actual channel availability still unverified.
  Native concurrent testing preferred over manually rotating artwork across different days.
- [YouTube tags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402): title/thumbnail/
  description matter more; tags mostly help spelling variants. Avoid tag stuffing.
- [YouTube monetisation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392): original,
  authentic work matters; mass-produced/repetitive template content risks eligibility.
  AI use alone is not a measured growth outcome. Rights, declarations, eligibility,
  advertiser suitability and actual earnings remain separate. Fictional gunfire/animal
  deaths in No Further Claims need a specific review; do not disguise them to evade it.
- [TikTok account types](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/growing-your-audience/switching-to-a-creator-or-business-account):
  Personal supports creator features; Business has different music access. Recommend
  Personal provisionally for this author/artist account, rather than switching for
  appearance. If primary purpose becomes selling services/products, reassess TikTok's
  Business guidance. Actual account type, region, link availability and eligibility unknown.
- [Creator Rewards official overview](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program):
  personal account in eligible region,18+,10k followers and100k views/30days are stated
  entry conditions; original eligible videos over a minute. Current account/in-app
  eligibility must be rechecked. [Help comparison](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-is-the-creator-rewards-program-different-from-the-tiktok-creator-fund)
  supports original quality/length distinction. Several direct TikTok Help pages returned
  no readable body in this session; do not invent complete current regional terms.
- [Creator Search Insights](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/creator-search-insights): search
  topics/content gaps, region-dependent. Useful for discoverability language; not a
  reason to replace our artistic direction with unrelated trends.
- [TikTok AI labels](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content):
  inspect current requirements for actual generated visuals/audio; retain disclosure
  decision in each package. Direct body unreadable here; no blanket label or exemption
  claim. Music/source rights and whether a post promotes own books need per-post review.

## Revenue hypotheses, not forecasts

Track separately platform payouts if eligible, book-page interest/sales with valid
attribution, and commissioned/licensed original films. No RPM estimate, revenue forecast
or audience uplift asserted. Short art pieces can support discovery without qualifying
for a particular payout programme. Do not stretch loops/stills past a minute to chase
eligibility, buy engagement, or imply AI-assisted work is human-only. A link visit is
not a book sale; public views are not qualified paid views.
