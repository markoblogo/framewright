## Fixture and method

The prompt requested a 15-second 16:9 trailer at 24 fps with 13 specified cuts, readable kinetic type, a cobalt and ice-blue palette, exact character continuity and beat-synced sound. All six original files were decoded locally. Five have 360 frames and one has 361 at 24 fps. Flow is 1280×720, `seedace.mp4` is 852×480, and the two Astra and two `anti` files are 1920×1080. The decoded originals share the first 15.000 seconds.

Review uses source time at 3.50, 5.50, 10.50 and 13.50 seconds across all files. These are matched positions on the clock. When action starts early or late, the frame is still sampled at that position. In the film, the shared view is followed by paired half-speed replays. Source audio was muted for the edit and is not evaluated here. The requested 13-cut count was not manually verified across every clip.

| 3.50 s · FULL SPEED triptych | Flow shows clearly readable vertical “FULL SPEED” in two cells. The `seedace` frame shows duplicate words and broken lettering. Both `anti` frames leave the right cell without a visible character at this instant. | Check actual letters and pose count independently. | 5.50 s · JUMP | “JUMP” is readable in all six samples. The Astra pair has closely matched headline geometry, with flat linework in the first and more rendered volume in the second. The first `anti` uses a simplified drawing. The second uses a detailed action pose. | Inspect typography, character rendering and intended contact as different outcomes. A single frame cannot establish palm contact through the move. |
| 10.50 s · poster beat | Flow presents a montage with visibly numbered `01` and `02` designs. The `seedace` frame repeats `01–04` over multiple cells. Astra and `anti` pairs each show a prominent `02` composition with different character treatment between variants. | Check the number of distinct poster designs, their actual order and letter integrity over the entire interval. |
| 13.50 s · AO identity card | All six samples show a recognisable AO card. The two `anti` cards are visually alike, despite distinct action rendering earlier. Flow and `seedace` also land on different character scale and graphic details. | Compare the action pose and the final identity against the same reference. A clean final card alone cannot establish continuity. |
