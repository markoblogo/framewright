# FilmCraft portrait export in a mathematical explainer

21 November 2026. Follow-up to the [18-second landscape fixture](filmcraft-edit-reopen-2026-11-20.md). FilmCraft CLI 0.20.1 assembled seven video clips and two audio placements for [Half a Life by 18?](../productions/half-a-life-by-18.md). A fresh process reopened the saved portrait project and confirmed all nine placements. FilmCraft natively exported the initial-review 1080×1920, 30 fps H.264/AAC master on CPU in 32.52 seconds.

The first call rejected integer frameRate before rendering. The successful call sets portrait frame size and inherits frame rate from the timeline. Decode, dimensions and 1466 frames passed. Six encoded samples were within 1.17 mean absolute RGB values of the corresponding inputs. Body audio correlation rounded to 1.0000. Two encoded sting samples matched the portrait source. Motion and typography were authored outside FilmCraft. The initial cut came back for pacing and picture revisions. The owner accepted the subsequent FFmpeg v2 picture edit. No separate FilmCraft tool evaluation was recorded. Portability, relative relink, desktop UI and interchange remain open. No paid API; active labour and compute cost unmeasured.
