## Production choices

- New shots are intentionally rough: native960×540,12fps,8 Eevee samples. The
  assembled review is720p24fps by scaling/repeating frames; this does not establish
  native720p or24fps motion quality for those new shots.
- Accepted pilot v5 remains the complete7.5s shot at18–25.5s. Its encoded video
  packets are copied without retiming/reframing. Original WAV is reused at unity
  gain; the broader newly authored sound bed is absent over that interval.
- Original cassette, reservoir and supports join the retained wide-v7 set. The
  cassette keeps the same seed geometry/scale and stays as an empty device after
  release. An actual socket recess replaces the early button-like markers.
- Reservoir view moves around the lamp that blocked the first control; no extra
  early falling bead is shown, avoiding its disappearance at the pilot cut.
- Dawn uses the actual translucent/exterior set, rather than the earlier unglazed
  scene. Controls show warm light reaching leaves/soil and window shadows. This
  is authored alpha transparency and lighting, not validated refraction/biology.

## Owner revision after full viewing

29October: owner rejected the overall slow pace, egg-like grain, artificial
reservoir/feed construction and weak captions. Falling, water, germination,
lighting change and final fade were the strongest moments to preserve in action.
The new selected working edit is40s:3/4/3/7.5/5/7.5/5/5 seconds. This shortening
is an operator response to the request for speed, not a new duration approval.

An original pointed flattened grain mesh with matte longitudinal husk texture
replaces the glossy ellipsoid across cassette, falling and mature shells. The
new hull requires a lower ground offset and matching runoff trajectory; growth
and camera timing of the7.5s sprout action remain. Original accepted pilot bytes
are preserved separately; its creative acceptance does not transfer to the new
hull/prop render.

The irrigation redesign has a rounded transparent reservoir offset from the
seed, thin collars/supports, smooth curved tubing and a small T-handle valve.
A lateral reservoir camera replaces an initially lamp-obscured view. The shallow
cassette is supported from the bench instead of a tall central rectangular rig.
New captions use larger bold light type on dark panels. Rendering remains an
explicitly rough local workflow; whole-film acceptance remains pending.
