## Question and boundary

Can selected simple film types go from one upfront agreement to complete YouTube
and TikTok publication packages, without intermediate owner approval, downloads,
window preparation, supplied files mid-run or other operator assistance?
The owner asked for 3–5 tests, initially simple, covering a few styles/tools; final
corrections should become scoped rules so later productions do not repeat them.
The owner also selected developing the speech-export solution or trying the tablet
browser/app route, rather than abandoning quality voices to avoid a download step.

This is autonomy **within the agreed production**, not permission for public upload,
a new paid/account step or resuming paused projects. Routine progress reports are
not input requests. Publication remains an owner action unless separately authorised.
The normal proposal/pilot approval loop is deliberately replaced for each admitted
test by the single upfront brief; internal pilots and self-correction may continue
without asking the owner to choose or approve intermediate versions.

## Agree once, before starting a test

Freeze the topic/source facts and viewer result; target duration/tolerance and
language; visual style/complexity; YouTube 16:9 and TikTok 9:16 layout/crop approach;
voice/music/branding; available assets/rights; eligible tools/accounts/hardware;
allowed resources, fallback routes and time/cost limits; and final package contents.
Resolve needed entitlement/access/export capabilities before the autonomous run.
If setup or asset preparation needs the owner, record that effort separately and
retain it in total workload; do not hide manual generation of all narration in setup.
An existing owner-supplied asset can be an upfront input, but then qualify only the
assembly-from-prepared-inputs scope, not autonomous creation of that asset.

Start the production clock after that agreement. Execute the agreed route and
permitted alternatives without stage questions. If no authorised route can finish,
retain the evidence and report the blocked result rather than requesting a rescue
or exceeding the agreed scope. A blocked run is useful evidence, not a success.

## Test2 proposal — One Light, One Way Home

Originally proposed15November2026 as a painted2.5D story. The owner selected the same story **in a near-photographic style**, then reaffirmed autonomous decisions. [Both56-second packages are delivered](../productions/one-light-one-way-home.md); baseline motion rejected, revision2 accepted; YouTube/TikTok publication URLs owner-reported in the case record. One bounded executed trial is now recorded under M3/M4; variants/retries are not new trials. The original painted treatment below was superseded for this case.

**Viewer result:** a small, complete story about finding the way home. At sea after dark, one small boat looks for a safe approach; a lighthouse beam finds it, the boat follows the light and reaches the warm harbour. End on the lighthouse above the sheltered boat. No dialogue, recognizable real location or factual claim is needed.

**Selected treatment:** original photorealistic generated stills with local2.5D motion, in navy, teal and warm amber. The painted/cut-paper texture proposal was superseded by the owner. Four/five motivated views: wide rough sea and distant light; boat nearer the water; beam/boat relationship; passing the harbour entrance; warm quiet arrival. Animate layered waves, spray, drifting mist, beam, boat pitch/bow wake/reflection and camera movement. These actions carry the story across the full film; one repeating pan over one unchanged picture is insufficient.

**Executed brief:**45–60seconds, final56; English Fola through the observed tablet route; YouTube16:9 and TikTok9:16 composed for each aspect; soft original music plus sea/wind/lighthouse sound. Complete cover/copy/subtitle/credit/download packages; one upfront agreement, then no intermediate owner approvals/downloads. No new purchase, paid API, account grant or public upload. Branding stays optional and unselected until the brief is accepted.

**Route and prior evidence:** original photorealistic generated stills, then local masked water/haze/light/camera compositing via Three.js180/Chrome plus Pillow/NumPy/FFmpeg using existing runtimes. Reuse [test1](../productions/same-street-three-moods.md)'s speech-export recovery, sound/packaging/check procedure and [Pantheon](../productions/pantheon-chatgpt-orb.md)'s local layered-camera/transition practice. That practice does not qualify this boat/water/beam sequence. A full3D build is a retained alternative, not selected for this photo-like2.5D test.

**Internal risk check:** boat/water contact must not look like a sliding sticker; beam, glow and the boat's location must agree; a small portrait boat must remain recognizable above the platform overlay area. Test a short linked beam-to-approach passage internally before full export; recompose/simplify locally within the accepted story if needed. Apply VT-MOTION/READ/FRAME/VOICE/COVER and inspect both complete encoded versions/reduced-size views. Owner review occurs only after complete delivery.

**Measurement improvement from test1:** timestamp the first action before preflight, label the accepted-brief timestamp UNKNOWN if unavailable, retain retries/generation counts and distinguish active work from waits. No total-labour or causal style-speed comparison from two different stories. Final feedback and any repeated defect become scoped observations, not invented new rules. Existing prepared tablet access remains a prerequisite; do not count needed owner setup as autonomous work.

**Bounded tablet-browser proof completed, 14 November:** one English Fola WAV was

It adds one bounded repeat/full-production instance; new languages, Knox selection and cold-session recovery remain untested. Mac/app alternatives remain candidates.
Retain [natural-voice policy](../voice-selection.md) and [Fola preference](../ukrainian-voice-baseline.md).
Do not silently substitute a rejected mechanical voice to manufacture autonomy.

| Candidate | What must actually work |
| --- | --- |
| Mac | Supported speech-generation/download path saves a usable local file without owner clicks; any developed helper respects the host/provider's permitted access paths. |
| Tablet browser | The supported browser workflow saves the chosen speech on Android, then a permitted transfer delivers the actual file to the Mac without owner assistance. |
| Android app | First establish a legitimate suitable app and its export/access/resource fit; no claim that a native AI Studio app exists or works. Test actual generation/export/transfer, not merely installation or playback. |

Choose an eligible candidate before admitting a voice-dependent production. Check
actual file existence, duration/decoding and expected content, transfer identity
where bytes should match, audible quality and the absence of human rescue. Listen
to the chosen route/take; an export success does not establish performance quality.
[LocalSend](android-companions-2026-11-13.md) has not achieved transfer on the tested
network; do not list it as working. Existing authorised USB/control options can be
candidates but must be tested for this exact export chain. No protection bypass,
credential extraction, purchase, app installation or new API authority follows
from this plan. API/app alternatives need their own actual upfront access decision.

## Deliver and measure each complete test

Deliver one complete MP4 per requested aspect, matching covers, English copy if
English was selected (otherwise the selected language), credits/source notes,
subtitles where requested, an accessible download/review handoff and concise QA.
Covers, subtitles and audio are part of the run, not a later manual packaging task.
No mid-run "download this", focus-window request or missing-artifact handoff can be
excluded from the intervention count. Do not silently shorten or simplify the
agreed story to call an incomplete package ready for publication.

Record actual observations in that production's existing journal/receipt:

- Upfront owner/setup effort; active operator work; waiting/render/export time;
  time from agreed start to complete delivery; correction/retry work; known costs
  and UNKNOWN amounts. A render duration is not total labour or money saved.
- Every owner intervention requested or supplied after start, with its cause;
  count zero only if there were genuinely none. Agent self-corrections are allowed
  within scope, but retain them and their resource/time cost.
- Requested versus delivered outputs, encoded QA and viewing/listening coverage;
  source/rights gaps and which tools/settings/version actually produced the result.
- Final owner quality judgment and correction time after complete delivery,
  separately from the mid-run intervention count. A technically complete package
  is not automatically creatively accepted, published or audience-validated.

A successful autonomous-delivery test requires the complete agreed packages,
observed relevant technical checks and **zero mid-run owner interventions**.
Quality is evaluated separately by the owner. If substantial changes are needed,
do not label the route publication-ready merely because it ran unattended.

## Learn between completed tests

Take final owner feedback once the package exists, at the owner's convenience;
it is not an intermediate production gate. Link corrections to applicable taste
IDs, preserve exceptions and name the missed check. Apply available feedback before
the next test; do not claim a learning effect if no feedback was received.
Report repeated defect categories and final review/correction effort without inventing
a score. If fewer than three tests finish, report a partial experiment.

After 3–5 tests, retain only the styles/routes that delivered adequate quality with
acceptable observed effort and no hidden assistance. Document conditions, failure
paths, fallback and unknowns; one film is not blanket tool qualification. These
results can inform a later owner-scoped release, but do not reopen REL-D, Quixote,
RUN or SSI or create a release obligation. No trial/film evidence count is added
until actual corresponding work has been conducted.
