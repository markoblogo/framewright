approximately three-minute adaptation. Its core is escalating dark satire:
a reward exchange becomes dependency and eventually canine control of humanity.
The bleak ending matters. Do not turn it into the earlier hopeful dog/light film
or imply the source contains the trilogy's later LitRPG mechanics or hero details.

request only relevant changed details: protagonist/appearance, location, chronology,
world rules and the chosen episode. Full manuscripts become useful if the owner
wants the adaptation to represent the trilogy rather than this original story.
