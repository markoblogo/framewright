## Five quality observations

- Readability: heading and lighthouse silhouette remain readable in inspected native and 480×270 frames; comparison labels are separate from the artwork.
- Motion: common light animation plus a discrete three-drawing, 10 Hz contour cycle; owner rejects the resulting vibration as unnatural for the stationary lighthouse.
- Drawing stability: same-frame contact/registration patches pass after the anchor repair. No human anatomy, acting or arbitrary drawing qualification.
- Artifacts: initial contact drift retained as a failure; fixed raw controls and encoded samples checked. Lossy codec pixels outside the raw contour are not claimed bit-identical.
- Control: exact redraw variants, seed, timing, anchor zones, source files and independent-frame reproduction retained. No automated tracing or arbitrary-image adaptation tested.
