## Decision and limits

**Passed for a curated portable planning recipe.** Useful when a recurring series benefits from fixed/variable boundaries,review rules and a shareable recipe. Not a one-click VIDEO.md→finished reusable video system. No configured analyzer,provider generation,renderer,media selection,NLE import or full recurring-series validation. Native skeleton retains its generic general_video preset entrypoint; draft status,no configured roles and explicit planning-only authority do not qualify it as a functioning offline renderer. Do not run the preset merely because a ZIP validates.

Do not make Capsule obligatory for one-off reviews. Use a curated recipe on a later owner-selected comparable production only if it improves the brief; actual reuse effort,owner preference and benefit remain unmeasured.
