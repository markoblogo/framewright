# One Random Life — a complete browser life in 118 seconds

An English 16:9 review of [1 in 8 Billion](https://1in8b.life/en/), using one
first-draw manual playthrough from birth to death, cropped native UI, selected
motion fragments and five illustrative photorealistic portrait stages.
[Sanitized evidence](../../experiments/2026-10-25/evidence/one-random-life.json) ·
[Output hashes](../../experiments/2026-10-25/outputs.json).

Anita Gautam’s fictional life begins in rural Odisha in 2026 and ends at 85 in 2111.
The film follows school, university, work, family, hospital costs, diabetes,
promotion, housing and later life. No rerolls, code/save manipulation or invented
game outcomes. The native16x speed and manual choices shorten the session. Capture
uses bounded browser screencast bursts and screenshots, not one continuous record.
The site’s statistical coverage is attributed to its creators, not independently
audited or treated as a future forecast.

Five generated stages—6,18,40,70,85—share an identity reference. Introductory and

publication copy disclose fictional AI illustrations, not documentary photographs
or game assets. A separate text-bearing cover was inspected full-size and 320px.
Six built-in image-generation calls including the cover; provider cost not reported.
No external paid generation, site purchase or new model download.

Cached Supertonic3 M1 supplies ten English phrases. An original 72 BPM sample-free
piano/pad score is mixed separately and ducked. Two narration phrases were revised
to remove an age 58-to-57 flashback. Subtitles are verbatim proportional chunks,
not forced-aligned; timing review remains open.

## Bounded OCR experiment and corrections

Tesseract 5.5.1 retrieved 9 of 11 exact phrases from five English source screenshots.
The two school phrases missed in a transient screenshot. A retained follow-up
found both in neighboring settled frames; frame 585 replaced the original.
OCR also retrieved the child’s pneumonia/recovery/bedside result at frame 1470.
Keep the initial 9/11 result: follow-up repair is not a fresh independent 11/11 test.
This demonstrates source retrieval for these frames, not semantic video review.

A background capture pump failed; synchronous bounded bursts worked. One native
transition contained tiny UI despite normal image dimensions, so a later stable
frame was used. The university crop moved upward to retain its heading. These
failures establish why accessible text and image dimensions alone cannot certify
a usable filmed frame.
