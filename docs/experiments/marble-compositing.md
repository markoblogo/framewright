# The Same Forest: a virtual world used as a film plate

24 October 2026. **42s film published by the owner:** [How Color Changes a Scene | One Forest, Three Feelings](https://youtu.be/wHOpypqPyCs). Watch-page title and42s player were independently observed. Publication settings, monetization, upload-byte identity and audience performance were not verified.

## What this pass established

An existing Marble 1.0 forest can supply a fixed plate for a separately animated original character, local colour treatment and a coordinated soundtrack. The finished horizontal film has an opening, cold isolation → amber shelter → red unease, a stop/turn/lantern action and a closing card. It is **locked-camera 2.5D compositing**, not an exported world mesh, calibrated relighting or a character walking inside a reconstructed 3D set.

The [retained English publication text](../../examples/marble-color-study/publication/youtube.md), optional descriptive captions and credits are published as text; no binary media is bundled.

The [original asset-dependent example](../../examples/marble-color-study/README.md) preserves the scene, deterministic frame recorder and sound builder. The [retained output record](../../experiments/2026-10-24/outputs.json) pins the final/native files; [bounded evidence](../../experiments/2026-10-24/evidence/marble-color-study.json) records checks and limitations. Media, account IDs, private project journal, original attempts and third-party runtime files stay outside Git. Generic example wrappers are sanitised and syntax-checked, not a new clean-machine render test.

## Source and production route

1. Three existing owner worlds were inspected: moonlit forest, Soviet apartment courtyard and rustic magical hut. The forest supplied path, moonlight, village windows and middle-distance trunks. No new world generation ran.
2. Studio’s native **Record in new project** imported the forest. The new Studio project was immediately set private and its disabled Share / Make public state observed. Native fixed-camera1080p30 video export worked; splat/collider/mesh download controls were disabled on the observed Free account. No bypass, upgrade or hidden asset endpoint was used.
3. The initial export was7.933s. A later open-angle Ultra export supplied a visually inspected frame at 3s. A tighter16:9 crop and soft shadow toe made the forest readable. Nominal1080p does not establish native1080p detail.
4. An original articulated Three.js r186 traveler was layered over that plate, with procedural gait, approximate contact shadow, lantern and drifting mist. The initial rear route used a hand-traced trunk holdout; the finished foreground route passes in front of the middle-distance trees and does not apply that old mask.
5. The final local recorder used absolute-time seeking and1260 JPEG94 frames fed to FFmpeg, rather than relying on real-time capture under rendering load. The native cover uses three treatments of the actual scene; scene captions are suppressed for that capture.

## Creative iterations and retained failures

- **8s initial sample:** owner rejected darkness and barely visible traveler. The cursor was a preview distraction, not encoded. Two corrective real-time attempts failed duration guards; one encoded6.641s. Failed/partial files remain retained.
- **12s image pass:** owner accepted improved forest/traveler and the three emotional states, while noting monotonous straight walking. Aggressive lifting of the first decoded/denoised frame had exposed blocky shadows; an inspected3s frame plus soft-toe grade worked better. Ultra alone did not fix the symptom; its exact cause was not isolated. An intermediate take also had elevated feet. PNG frame capture was slow; a partial run was stopped and retained.
- **12s sound/acting pass:** the owner accepted the music direction and felt the anxiety, but found cold/warm separation weak. The actor gained a stop, turn and lantern raise. The accepted short file remains unchanged.
- **42s final assembly:** stronger golden/smoothed middle state, opening/ending, longer arrangement and unarchived English YouTube kit. The owner subsequently published the film. This is a publication outcome, not a separate final-master listening attestation or audience result.

The successful12s JPEG job took46.044s in its observed environment. Other source/render conditions changed, so this is not a controlled PNG-versus-JPEG benchmark or measured production time saving.

## Sound and finishing

Retained licensed Dream Walk sources were reused: benpm’s **Ambient Piano Music #4** (CC BY4.0) and felix.blume’s **Night close to a river, crickets and frogs, bird sometimes.** (CC0). Source hashes, verbatim attribution and adaptation notes are retained privately and in the delivered credits. The piano receives coordinated sparse/cold, fuller/warm and low/tense arrangements. Original procedural footsteps, low wind/tension textures and lantern handling follow the picture cues. The field recording is a stylised fiction bed, not sound recorded from the virtual world.

A common gain restored premix headroom without changing relative stem levels. The longer mix needed an explicit44.1→48kHz resample before rate-based octave/time processing; the earlier full mix is retained, the accepted short sample unchanged. The corrected final master was remuxed and checked again.

## Reusable lessons and next gate

- Select and inspect the decoded plate before deep grading; an export preset or first frame alone is insufficient.
- Couple colour and sound cues on one timeline. Strengthen visual differentiation before extending a representative sample.
- Prefer deterministic capture when load breaks real-time duration; preserve the failed route instead of weakening its guard.
- Verify source sample rate before rate-based pitch processing; technical measurements and human listening are different evidence.
- Keep a new final/master/cover independent from earlier approvals and retain publication as its own receipt.

The remaining creative limits are the soft old environment, simple procedural actor and one fixed composition. The next useful production proof is a short interaction across two shots with character/ground/occlusion continuity. Mesh/depth access or another provider should be introduced only if that shot requires it, with its own authority and rights check. Pocket OS and SSI weekly work remain paused.
