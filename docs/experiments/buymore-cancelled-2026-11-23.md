# BuyMore — Your Bus Has Been Cancelled

23 November 2026. One YouTube-first editorial pilot, 22 seconds. The test asks
whether a product photo and one status change carry a complete joke.

The [original BuyMore production](../productions/buymore-problems-delivered.md)
supplied the store identity, monitor plate, Indoor Bus Stop photo and shopper
voice. The sequel uses two new fictional reviews, an animated board within
the photo, a new sound mix, a two-unit quantity change, and a fictional
payment confirmation for $2,998.00. The score drops out under the countdown.
The buyer hears the cancellation as a reason to miss work and orders two,
including one for his wife. All checkout states are authored locally.

The saved HTML scene was captured locally in Chrome and encoded at 1280×720,
24 fps with English Kokoro am_michael dialogue, music and effects. Both
streams decoded in full. Encoded samples show the monitor, both board states,
the quantity change, order review and confirmation. The owner accepted the landscape cut and
requested TikTok and publication packaging.

Private editable scene, mix, render recipe and review are under
`productions/buymore-pilot/cancelled/`. The original storefront media are
shared sources.

## Publication package

The landscape MP4 was carried into the package byte for byte. The 1080×1920
portrait version opens on the reused smartphone plate and moves into a
single-column storefront. It uses tap indicators and in-picture English
dialogue captions. Both cuts run 22 seconds and share the same source mix.
The portrait media fully decoded. Encoded samples show the sign and the
checkout beats without caption overlap. Both covers use the same shelter
photo and restage the cancellation board for their aspect. YouTube has a
separate English SRT, three title choices, description and tags. TikTok has
a caption and first comment. Discovery is recommendations-led; a small public
YouTube search informed vocabulary, with no demand estimate. Public TikTok
search was unavailable.
