# Prayer rings · two local tool trials

21 November 2026. [Production](../productions/prayer-rings.md). Active labour, hardware throughput and audience outcomes were unmeasured. Local Mac runs without paid calls.

## OpenMotion: data-driven notification animation

Reused retained OpenMotion `fcbaa2b`, Motion Package v0.2. Authored an original HTML/Canvas insert driven by a separately inventoried JSON file. Lock, pack and relocated check passed with five files. The portable copy rendered 96 frames at 30 fps in each 960×540 and 540×960 layout with Chromium/FFmpeg. Frame hashes matched before and after seeking back to 1.25 s in each aspect. Text and the stacked/row signal paths were inspected at reduced sizes. The original fixed title remains readable; the animated pulse travels independently of text. No native Studio MP4 export or cross-host test. Both exported inserts were used in the owner-accepted v1 cuts. The inserts received no separate owner verdict.

## ThreeRT: scene binding on an original generic ring

First bounded authoring test on published `@threert/scene` 0.1.1. Native TorusGeometry and PlaneGeometry, two materials, scene lighting, camera descriptor and named ring were constructed. RecordingEngine registered two geometries and two materials. Rotating the ring emitted one transform and no geometry update, while the next unchanged sync emitted no calls. The first assertion expected explicit light/camera calls; actual binding registered the environment and returned camera settings via the separate camera descriptor. This exercised scene authoring, with native ray-traced pixels, GPU performance, export and three.js comparisons still open. The test scene was kept out of the edit.

Retained private evidence: `motion-source`, `motion-reopened`, `qa/motion-*.json*`, `threert-trial.mjs` and `qa/threert-scene.json`.
