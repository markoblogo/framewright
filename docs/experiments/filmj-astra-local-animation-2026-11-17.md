## Owner decision

The owner accepted the selected images for a teaser, then requested moving character scenes, English speech, minimal music and sound effects. The16s call-scene trial was explicitly rejected. Owner assessment: this did not improve on the awkward local animation already available before the model switch. No controlled comparison against another model was performed. The owner's report of enthusiastic Astra video reviews was the reason for trying it, not verified capability evidence.

## Executed scope

137 local image files,135 unique byte hashes were indexed from the supplied filmJ folder. A27s landscape montage (26.708333s) used photo camera moves, comic panels and a locally synthesised score. This established selection/direction only. A16s960x540,24fps call scene added local Pillow mesh deformation of face/shoulders/lips, a procedural illustrated hand and rotating spiral eyes. Three English Kokoro voices, a quiet synthetic bed and phone tones were mixed locally. The16s scene did not meet owner requirements for convincing character animation.

These were authored Python/Pillow/NumPy animations encoded with FFmpeg, with Kokoro-ONNX speech. Native text-to-video or image-to-video inference by Astra was not executed. The session model label is contextual, not a separately measured video renderer/version. The earlier confident delivery language overstated creative success relative to the actual limited deformation.

## Quality and limits

- Readability: selected plates accepted;complete teaser/acting approval absent.
- Motion: owner rejected the character-motion result;local deformation did not meet the requested animated-scene standard.
- Drawing stability: no systematic frame-by-frame identity assessment;no demonstrated qualification.
- Artifacts: procedural hand was visibly schematic;deformed face/mesh needs manual scrutiny;no numerical artifact score.
- Controllability: explicit local timeline and deformation code retained;convincing acting remains unsolved for this application.

Both MP4s completed full FFmpeg decode without reported errors. This verifies file execution only. Active work time,controlled time savings and comparative model advantage are unknown. No paid provider/API generation was used;local compute unpriced. Publication rights for the collected artwork remain unverified. Neither trial was published.
