## Reuse with and without Framewright

With Framewright: resume the existing brief/journal, retain source identities and
consent, map scenes to picture/speech, pin the two outputs, record range/defect/change/
protected elements, inspect corrected context and retain final receipts. Neither
retention nor a technical pass marks the owner review complete.

Without Framewright: keep the same brief, scene table, private source/sanitised asset
split, consent record, native Motion Canvas/Blender projects, version lockfiles,
