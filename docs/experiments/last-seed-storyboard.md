## Continuity and cut rules

- **palette:** Retain pilot teal structure, brass tray/nozzle, ochre seed and green leaves; dawn adds warm light
- **growth:** Same retained seed, stem and leaves, same scale. No new growing cycle afterS04
- **geography:** Cassette above tray; nozzle connected to supply; window direction fixed throughout
- **motion:** Small motivated camera/leaf/dust movement; no long frozen holds
- **source_policy:** Original local geometry/materials and sound; free tools on Mac, heavy jobs sequential

All cuts use one timeline:1440frames. Fade/title time is included. The final output target is native720p initially; a1080p re-render is a separate quality decision. Avoid enlarging the approved720p pilot while calling it native1080p.

## Cheap checks before full rendering

- **LIGHT-01** — 2s lamp-to-dawn light/shadow transition on saved set. Pass: Readable causal change without clipping, flicker or incoherent shadow.
- **CONTINUITY-01** — Macro/wide control stills from same saved seed/tray/set revision. Pass: Matching shape, scale, position, pipe geography and leaf identity.
- **RECOVERY-R03** — Resume one retained shot after a fresh session and compare controls. Pass: Correct source/settings, no duplicate dispatch, selected correction isolated.

PREVIS-01 is an optional three-still comparison for the wide reveal; its variants have not been rendered or selected. DJV is a possible repeat test, not a prerequisite or a completed trial. Tone.js is not needed to preserve the accepted soundtrack; any new synthesis comparison must justify its role.

## Production route and remaining decisions

Use the existing original Blender scene, add cassette/reservoir/lamp/wide surroundings, retain new versions and check macro/wide controls. Render sequentially with measured local Eevee fallback; its admitted executor support is still absent. Assemble with FFmpeg and mix the accepted pilot segment with an original broader bed. The measured pilot render is a feasibility observation, not a reliable full-film time quote: wider sets and new lighting need a short cost probe.

Next deliverable after storyboard selection: cheap wide/set and light-transition controls, then a rough timed animatic. Do not render60seconds before these checks. Keep pilot-only and full-film approvals distinct.

Final local handoff later: MP4, English title/description/tags, thumbnail **with text**, subtitles only if spoken content is introduced, and checks report. YouTube publication remains a separate owner action.

This storyboard does not close R03 or REL-A. Full film, final review, fresh-session continuation and delivery kit remain.
